# Changelog

Every Impel Down release for Minecraft 1.20.1, newest first, as published on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1812558).

## 0.1.0 (beta) { #v0-1-0 }

<small>Released 2026-11-17 · [Download](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1812558)</small>

<div class="changelog-body" markdown="0">
<p><strong>The first beta: the World Government's great prison under the sea.</strong> Find it in the deep ocean behind its Gate of
Justice, or lose a fight to the Marines as a pirate and wake up in a cell. Things may still change, and bugs are
likely: please report them.</p>
<p>Requires Minecraft <strong>1.20.1</strong>, Forge <strong>47.4.2</strong> or newer, <strong>Mine Mine no Mi 0.11.6</strong> and <strong>AkumaLib 6.8.1</strong> or newer.
Needed on both the client and the server.</p>
<hr />
<ul>
<li><strong>Getting in</strong>: prisons stand in the deep oceans, each behind its Gate of Justice, with a pier for your boat. Or get
  arrested: a pirate or revolutionary beaten by a marine or a bounty hunter is jailed on a level set by his bounty.</li>
<li><strong>Seven levels</strong>: the entrance (level 0), then Crimson Hell, Wild Beast Hell, Starvation Hell, Blazing Hell,
  Freezing Hell and Eternal Hell, each with its own land, beasts and dangers: blade trees, the Basilisk and the
  Sphinx, hunger, a lake of fire, the cold.</li>
<li><strong>Guards</strong> stronger the deeper you go: jailers, riflemen, hooded guards, a head jailer on levels 1 to 6, the four demon
  guards, the chief of the guard and the warden. Surveillance snails raise the alarm.</li>
<li><strong>Keys and lifts</strong>: a cell key per level, the master key, and the lift key to ride to any level whose head
  jailer you have beaten.</li>
<li><strong>Prisoners</strong> live in their cells. Open a cell and they make a run for the great gate.</li>
<li><strong>In jail</strong>: your gear waits in your level's depot. Serve your time, or escape: leave by the great gate and
  your bounty grows.</li>
<li><strong>Loot</strong>: chests richer the deeper, and Devil Fruit boxes, one always on level 6.</li>
<li><strong>The tower resets</strong> some time after the last player leaves.</li>
<li>Server settings in <code>serverconfig/inoimpel-server.toml</code>, and the <code>/impel</code> command for operators.</li>
</ul>
</div>
