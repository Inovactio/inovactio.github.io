Found in the base mod's **iron Devil Fruit box**. Eat it and its abilities appear in the ability menu, grouped under the fruit.

!!! note "About the values"
    The values are those of the ability's tooltip in game, with the base mod's default settings. Damage is shown on the base mod's scale, as in the ability screen.

## Netsuryo Up { #netsuryo-up }

![](../abilities/netsuryo-up.png){ .ability-icon } *Active · Transformation*

The user raises their body heat to 3,000, 5,500 or 10,000 degrees. The hotter they are, the more their other abilities hurt and the further ice and snow melt around them, but the shorter it lasts.

Enemies standing close catch fire and so do the ones who hit the user with their hands or a weapon. At 10,000 degrees allies burn too, and the stone under the user's feet turns to lava for a while.

The **Alt key** picks: **3,000 Degrees**, **5,500 Degrees** or **10,000 Degrees**

| Stat | 3,000 Degrees | 5,500 Degrees | 10,000 Degrees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Element | Fire | Fire | Fire |
| Haki | Special | Special | Special |
| Range | 2.5 blocks (area) | 2.5 blocks (area) | 4 blocks (area) |
| Other Abilities' Damage | +25 % | +50 % | +100 % |
| Melting | 3 | 5 | 8 |
| Burns Who Hits the User | 3 s | 3 s | 5 s |
| Duration | 60 s | 30 s | 12 s |
| Damage | — | — | 2 |
| Damage to Who Hits the User | — | — | 2 |
