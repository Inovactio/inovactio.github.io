# Missing Missing no Mi

![](../../assets/icons/missing-missing-no-mi.png){ .mod-icon }

**Missing Missing no Mi** adds to [Mine Mine no Mi](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-mod) the **Devil Fruits of *One Piece* the base mod does not have yet**: canon fruits from the manga, and non-canon fruits from the anime, the films, the video games and the novels. Every fruit keeps its name and its techniques from the work.

| | |
|---|---|
| Version documented | **1.0.0** |
| Mod id | `inomissing` |

## What it adds

**21 Devil Fruits**: 15 Paramecia, 2 Logia and 4 Zoan, with their abilities, transformations, summoned creatures and status effects.

The fruits behave like the base mod's own:

- they **drop from the base mod's Devil Fruit boxes**, each in the box matching its tier, and are drawn as often as a base mod fruit;
- the base mod's **one-fruit-per-world rule** applies to them unchanged;
- they appear in the base mod's **Devil Fruits** creative tab.

Nothing here replaces or rebalances the base mod, and a fruit Mine Mine no Mi has itself is never added twice.

## Getting started

1. Install Forge for Minecraft 1.20.1, then put **Mine Mine no Mi**, **AkumaLib** and **Missing Missing no Mi** in your `mods` folder.
2. Find a fruit in a Devil Fruit box. Wooden, iron and golden boxes each hold the fruits of their tier, listed in [Fruits](fruits/index.md).
3. Eat it. Its abilities appear in the base mod's ability menu, grouped under the fruit.
