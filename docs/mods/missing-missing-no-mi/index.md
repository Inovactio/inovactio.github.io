# Missing Missing no Mi

![](../../assets/icons/missing-missing-no-mi.png){ .mod-icon }

**Missing Missing no Mi** adds to [Mine Mine no Mi](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-mod) the **Devil Fruits of *One Piece* the base mod does not have yet**: canon fruits from the manga, and non-canon fruits from the anime, the films, the video games and the novels. Every fruit keeps its name and its techniques from the work.

## What it adds

**74 Devil Fruits**: 51 Paramecia, 5 Logia and 18 Zoan (5 of them ancient, 5 mythical), with their abilities, transformations, summoned creatures and status effects.

The fruits behave like the base mod's own:

- they **drop from the base mod's Devil Fruit boxes**, each in the box matching its tier, and are drawn as often as a base mod fruit;
- the base mod's **one-fruit-per-world rule** applies to them unchanged;
- they appear in the base mod's **Devil Fruits** creative tab.

Nothing here replaces or rebalances the base mod, and a fruit Mine Mine no Mi has itself is never added twice.

## NPCs with these fruits

Mine Mine no Mi's **captains, notorious NPCs and troops** can draw every fruit of this addon and fight with most of their techniques, as they do with the base mod's own fruits.

- The weakest troops get the wooden box fruits.
- A Zoan takes its fighting form up close and its other form further off, and switches form when a technique of the other form is ready. The flying fruits fly on an NPC: it circles over you, dives on you and comes down to rest. The other flights are a short hop for an attack from the air.
- An Uta Uta NPC sings players into its dream, where its double fights them on the stage. A Hobi Hobi NPC makes toys that are free again after 2 minutes at most, or when it dies or leaves.
- An NPC's summons fight for it, and what it leaves behind (servants, caught shots, a borrowed look) is let go when it dies or leaves.
- Nothing of an NPC's fruit can be farmed: its gold, metal and turret bullets never run out, and its turret drops nothing.
- NPCs with these fruits break no blocks, unless the server turns on `npcAbilityGriefing` in `akumalib-server.toml` (off by default). Players are not affected.

## Getting started

1. Install Forge for Minecraft 1.20.1, then put **Mine Mine no Mi**, **AkumaLib** and **Missing Missing no Mi** in your `mods` folder.
2. Find a fruit in a Devil Fruit box. Wooden, iron and golden boxes each hold the fruits of their tier, listed in [Fruits](fruits/index.md).
3. Eat it. Its abilities appear in the base mod's ability menu, grouped under the fruit.
