# Getting around

Skypiea's islands lie two to four thousand blocks apart, and most of the way between two of them is the void. Three ways to cross it: the **waver** on the sea, the **Milky Roads** on foot, and the [flying mounts](wildlife.md#the-flying-mounts).

## The waver

Skypiea's cloud-sea scooter: a small wooden hull with a teal bow, a column with handlebars, and a **Breath Dial** for an engine.

<div class="model-viewer" data-models="../models/" data-ids="waver"></div>

- **One rider, standing** at the handlebars. Mobs are never scooped up, as a boat does.
- **Faster than a boat**: about 13 blocks a second, against 8 for a boat on the same sea.
- **It glides on cloud**: on the islands' cloud skin and on the Milky Roads it slides as on the sea, and it climbs steps of one block.
- A hard landing does not break it. Broken, it drops itself.

**Getting one**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Craft it** | an Iron Ingot over a **Breath Dial** between two Planks, over three Planks |
| **Buy it** | from the **Waver Dealer**, behind the counter of the seafront's dial workshop: 3 to 4 million extol. He also sells the base mod's dials. |

The Breath Dial is the base mod's: it washes up on [Angel Beach](angel-islands.md#angel-beach), and the dial merchants sell it.

## The Milky Roads

**Every island is linked to its nearest neighbour** by a road of **milky cloud**, five blocks wide, from centre to centre.

- **On the sea**, the road rides the swell, flush with the surface.
- **Over the void**, it is a bridge at the sea's level.
- **Reaching an island**, it climbs onto it by a ramp.
- It glows faintly at night.

A road stops short of what stands in its way — an island's cliffs, Heaven's Gate, a pier — and never crosses [Weatheria](weatheria.md). Roads appear in the parts of Skypiea explored with this version or later.

**Milky Cloud** and the **Milky Curb** along its edges are blocks: dig them with a shovel.
