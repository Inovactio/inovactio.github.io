# Advancements

Sky Island has an advancement tab of its own, **Sky Island**: a thread through Skypiea, in the order you meet it. **None gives a reward**. Three of them stay **hidden until you get them**: they are surprises.

## The way up and the Angel Islands

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Sky Island** | Ride a Knock-Up Stream up to Skypiea. |
| **Heaven's Gate** | Come to the gate at the top of the clouds. |
| **Express Shrimp** | Have your boat pulled up by the express shrimp at Heaven's Gate. |
| **A Shell With A Power** | Get a dial — any one, Sky Island's or the base mod's, however you get it. |
| **Waver** | Get a waver. |
| **Feathered Friend** | Tame a Three-Jo Bird or a Sky Dot Bird. |
| **Up In The Air** | Ride a bird you tamed. |
| **Cloud Pet** | Tame a Cloud Fox or a Giant Dog. |
| **Weatheria** | Find the floating town of the weather scientists. |
| **Birka** | Set foot on Birka, the island of Enel's birth. |

## The Upper Yards

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Upper Yard** | Set foot on the holy land of an Upper Yard. |
| **Giant Jack** (goal) | Climb to the top of the Giant Jack. |
| **The Bell Rings** | Ring the Golden Bell at its top. |
| **Shandora** | Find the ruins of Shandora. |
| **City Of Gold** (challenge, hidden) | Find Shandora whole and golden. |
| **Written In Stone** | Learn a priest's Ordeal from its stele. |
| **Satori**, **Shura**, **Gedatsu**, **Ohm Beaten** (goals) | Win each priest's Ordeal. A win in Hard counts too. |
| **Satori**, **Shura**, **Gedatsu**, **Ohm Beaten Hard** (challenges) | Win each priest's Ordeal in Hard. |
| **God Beaten** (challenge, hidden) | Beat Enel on the Ark Maxim. |
| **Great Serpent** (challenge, hidden) | Kill the great serpent of the Upper Yard. |
| **Sanctuary Of God** | Reach the sanctuary where Enel sits. |

## The way back

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Soft Landing** | Come down to the Blue Sea under a balloon octopus — one that left the sky with your boat or your ship. |
