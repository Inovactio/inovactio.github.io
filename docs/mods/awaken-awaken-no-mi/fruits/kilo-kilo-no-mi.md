# Kilo Kilo no Mi

**Paramecia** · 3 awakened abilities

These abilities unlock once the fruit is **awakened**: see [Awakening](../index.md#getting-started).

!!! note "About the values"
    The values are those of the ability's tooltip in game, with the base mod's default settings. Damage is shown on the base mod's scale, as in the ability screen.

## Gravity Zone { #gravity-zone }

*Active · Zone*

Creates a gravity field that makes allies lighter and enemies heavier.

Heavy enemies are crushed by their own weight at every pulse of the field.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cooldown | 120 s |
| Charge | 3–12 s |
| Zone Radius (blocks) | 128 |
| Effect Pulse (s) | 2.5 |
| Damage | 10 |

## Infinity Kilo Punch { #infinity-kilo-punch }

*Active · Punch*

Punches with infinite weight, dealing massive damage and launching the target really far away.

The target turns heavy in flight and slams back down once past the top of its arc.

**Stats while active**

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Speed | <span class="stat-malus">x0.7</span> |

## Weight Shift { #weight-shift }

*Passive*

In the air the user is light as a feather: blows hurt far less but send them flying, and falls deal no damage.

Crouched on the ground the user weighs ten thousand kilos and cannot be knocked back or launched.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Damage Reduction In The Air (%) | 50 |
| Knockback Taken In The Air (x) | 2 |
