## Nyorai Shinshō { #nyorai-shinsho }

*Active*

Drops a giant Buddha palm from the sky onto the aimed spot, crushing everything under it.

Anything it lands on is pinned to the ground for 40 seconds, unable to walk, swim or jump

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cooldown | 90 s |
| Charge | 1 s |
| Damage | 120 |
| Range | 14 blocks (area) |

## Sanctuary { #sanctuary }

*Active*

The Buddha stands over its own: every blow aimed at an ally nearby lands on the user instead.

The blow keeps its nature, so the user's armour is what decides the price. The vow breaks once they have taken enough.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cooldown | 90 s |
| Hold | 60 s |
| Shelters Allies Within (blocks) | 24 |
| Breaks After Taking (HP) | 300 |
