## Gear Fifth { #gear-fifth }

![](../abilities/gear-fifth.png){ .ability-icon } *Active · Transformation*

Applies ![](../effect-icons/flattened.png){ .effect-mini .pixelated }[Flattened](../effects.md#effect-flattened)

The user's heart takes up the Drums of Liberation and their body becomes as free as a drawing.

Blows squash them instead of breaking them, though blades still cut, and they can run through the air.

Takes the place of the other Gears.

While Gear Fifth is on, four abilities of the base mod change: **Pistol** becomes **Dawn Pistol**; **Gatling** becomes **Dawn Gatling**, 12 salvos of giant fists; **Bazooka** becomes **Dawn Cymbal**, two giant hands that clap the targets **Flattened** for 3 seconds and throw them; **Rocket** becomes **Dawn Rocket**, whose arm also catches enemies while the user slams into the first enemy on the way. They go back to what they were when the form ends.

The base mod's own awakened abilities of this fruit (its Gear Fifth, Gigant and Dawn Whip) are replaced by the ones on this page: they are taken off a player who had them.

## Gomu Gomu no Sekai { #gomu-gomu-no-sekai }

![](../abilities/gomu-gomu-no-sekai.png){ .ability-icon } *Active · Zone*

Turns the surroundings to rubber for a while.

A fall bounces instead of landing, without harm for the user and their allies, and the ground throws enemy projectiles back at whoever fired them.

Can only be used in Gear Fifth.
