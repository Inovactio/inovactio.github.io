## Juggernaut { #juggernaut }

![](../abilities/juggernaut.png){ .ability-icon } *Active*

The rhino charges where it looks and gets faster and faster, nothing stops it, not holds, not pushes and not walls it can break.

Enemies in its way get hit harder the faster it goes and are thrown aside. A wall it can't break ends the charge and leaves the rhino staggered.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cooldown | 45 s |
| Hold | 6 s |
| Damage | 40–120 |
| Breaks Blocks up to Hardness | 1.5 |
