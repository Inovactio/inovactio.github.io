<h4>A Few Highlights</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Trunk Geyser</strong> (Zou): a jet of seawater. A Devil Fruit user it soaks loses their powers for a moment, as if they had fallen into the sea</li>
<li><strong>Rebirth Blaze</strong> (Phoenix): the phoenix burns, untouchable, and is reborn at full health - the health it takes back erupts as a nova. The lower it was, the bigger the blast</li>
<li><strong>Thundering Herd</strong> (Bison): run a route, and a phantom stampede thunders down the whole of it</li>
<li><strong>Ancient Hide</strong> (Mammoth): in an awakened form, no single blow takes more than 15% of your health</li>
<li><strong>On the Beat</strong> (Oto): blows landed on the tempo build a damage combo</li>
<li><strong>Anesthesia</strong> (Ope): a numbed target's invulnerability frames stop protecting it from you and your side</li>
</ul>

<h3>Changes</h3>
<h4>Renamed abilities</h4>
<p>Awakened abilities no longer borrow the names of techniques the manga gives to the fruit's normal form:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Entei</strong> → <strong>Crown of Flames</strong>, and <strong>Entei Flight</strong> → <strong>Crown Flight</strong> (Mera Mera)</li>
<li><strong>Hiraishin</strong> → <strong>Stormstep</strong> (Goro Goro)</li>
<li><strong>Kirimanjaro</strong> → <strong>Longneck Ram</strong> (Ushi Ushi: Giraffe)</li>
<li><strong>Punk Corrida</strong> → <strong>Scrap Tempest</strong> (Jiki Jiki)</li>
<li><strong>Takamagahara</strong> → <strong>Lightfall Domain</strong> (Pika Pika)</li>
<li><strong>Yomi no Fubuki</strong> → <strong>Soulchill Aura</strong> (Yomi Yomi)</li>
</ul>
<h4>Reworked awakenings</h4>
<p>Every awakening that already existed was revisited when its fruit was extended. Among the larger changes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Damage raised</strong> across many abilities that were weaker than the fruit's own base kit - for example Drought Calamity, Ivory Catastrophe, Ao no Rakka, Horn Drill, Ancient Spine Saw and Slick Disarm</li>
<li><strong>Mechanics that were promised and missing now work</strong>: Ivory Catastrophe actually spins, Candle World burns those caught in its soft wax, Spin (Kame) blocks attacks, Battlefield Resonance applies its bonuses, Kenzan World can no longer be dodged by crouching</li>
<li><strong>Friend and foe sorted out</strong>: several zones no longer help enemies or hurt allies (Spring Domain, Waterpool Domain, Resonating World, High-Frequency Wall, Kenzan World)</li>
<li><strong>Fiddle Overdrive</strong> (Bison): no longer knocks you unconscious when it ends, and its steam finally shows</li>
<li><strong>Tooltips</strong>: many abilities now show their real numbers - heals, durations, cooldowns and bonuses that were missing</li>
<li><strong>Every ability description</strong> rewritten in the base mod's register</li>
<li><strong>Status effect icons</strong> redrawn as 18x18 pixel art, matching vanilla's</li>
</ul>
<h4>Sounds</h4>
<ul>
<li>Dozens of abilities that were silent now have their own sounds</li>
<li><strong>Zone charge sounds</strong> no longer play two or three copies of themselves on top of each other</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3>Fixes</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Interrupted charges</strong> (water, Seastone, handcuffs) no longer deliver the ability at full power, and no longer leave you rooted or a zone half-built</li>
<li><strong>Spring Domain</strong>: allies' jumps are no longer worse inside the field, bounces never lose their speed, and the zone costs a fraction of what it did</li>
<li><strong>Spring Fajin</strong>: charging in mid-air holds you in place instead of dropping you</li>
<li><strong>Drought Calamity</strong>: the charge animation no longer breaks in a released game, and the drought no longer drains caves deep under the field</li>
<li><strong>Beta Beta mucus</strong> no longer slows the server down the more of it exists</li>
<li><strong>Dislocated limbs</strong> hide correctly when both arms and legs are dislocated</li>
<li><strong>Awakening smoke</strong> sits on the neck of several forms that had it misplaced</li>
</ul>

## 1.1.0 { #v1-1-0 }

<div class="changelog-body" markdown="0">
<blockquote>
<h4>⚠️ AkumaLib update required: 2.2.0</h4>
<p>This version needs <strong><a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/akumalib" rel="nofollow">AkumaLib</a> 2.2.0</strong> or newer. With an older AkumaLib the game will stop on Forge's missing-dependency screen.</p>
<p>Just replace the AkumaLib jar in your <code>mods</code> folder with the new one.</p>

<p><strong>Every Zoan awakening is now complete.</strong> The awakened Zoans only covered one of their two forms. This release adds <strong>the twelve missing awakened forms</strong>, gives the <strong>Daibutsu</strong> its first real ability, and brings the mod to <strong>70 fruits and 148 awakened abilities</strong>.</p>
<hr>
<h3>New Content</h3>
<h4>Fruits Abilities</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hito Hito no Mi: Daibutsu</strong>: Added Nyorai Shinshō</li>
</ul>
<h4>Awakened Zoan Forms</h4>
<p>Each awakened Zoan now has both of its forms, not just one. Every new form has its own model, stats and icon, and follows the same calibration as the awakened form the fruit already had.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Walk Points</strong>: Leopard, Allosaurus, Axolotl, Bison, Giraffe and Sai</li>
<li><strong>Guard Points</strong>: Kame, Brachiosaurus, Mammoth and Zou</li>
<li><strong>Fly Points</strong>: Phoenix and Pteranodon</li>
</ul>
<h4>Nyorai Shinshō</h4>
<p>The Daibutsu awakening used to be just a form. <strong>Nyorai Shinshō</strong> brings the palm of the Buddha down from the sky onto the point you aim at, up to 64 blocks away:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>120 damage</strong> to everything within <strong>14 blocks</strong> of the impact</li>
<li>Everything it lands on is <strong>sealed into the ground for 40 seconds</strong>, unable to walk, swim or jump</li>
<li>Where the base Impact Blast strikes forward from the fist, this one comes from above, at range, and holds</li>
</ul>
<h4>Quality of Life</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Your fruit's own abilities work in the new forms</strong>: every base ability that needs a Walk, Guard or Fly Point also accepts its awakened version. Kame Rideable, Brachio Bomber, Ancient Sweep, Mammoth Trample, Fujiazami and Flames of Regeneration included</li>
<li><strong>Faster flight</strong>: the awakened Fly Points use the fruit's normal flight, only faster</li>
<li><strong>Awakened Kame Guard Point</strong>: crouching retracts into the shell, where almost nothing gets through</li>
<li><strong>Awakening smoke</strong>: on the new forms, the smoke around the neck follows the model's head</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3>Changes</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Glittering Storm</strong> (Kira Kira): the diamond tornado is no longer put out by rain. Water still stops it</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3>Fixes</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Titan Trample</strong> (Deka Deka), <strong>Spin</strong> (Kame Kame) and <strong>Brachio Stomp</strong> (Ryu Ryu: Brachiosaurus) no longer damage their own user</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3>Requirements</h3>
<ul>
<li>Minecraft <strong>1.20.1</strong></li>
<li>Forge <strong>47.4.18+</strong></li>
<li><strong>Mine Mine no Mi</strong> <code>1.20.1-0.11.5</code></li>
<li><strong>AkumaLib</strong> <code>2.2.0</code> — <strong>update required</strong></li>
<li><em>(Recommended)</em> <strong>Mine Mine no Mi: Awaken Path</strong> for survival progression</li>
</ul>
</div>

## 1.0.0 { #v1-0-0 }

<small>Released 2026-09-03 · [Download](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/awaken-awaken-no-mi/files/8799400)</small>

<li><strong>Magu Magu no Mi</strong>: Added Kagutsuchi and Netsuryo</li>
</ul>
<h4>Passive Abilities</h4>
<p>Ten of the new abilities are <strong>passives</strong> — they work on their own, with no key to press, and grow stronger the more you play to the fruit's strengths.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Storm Sovereign</strong> (Goro Goro): stronger during a thunderstorm</li>
<li><strong>Sun's Blessing</strong> (Pika Pika): stronger in bright light</li>
<li><strong>Kawaki</strong> (Suna Suna): stronger standing on sand and quicksand</li>
<li><strong>Ferrous Dominion</strong> (Jiki Jiki): stronger standing on your own scrap field</li>
<li><strong>Zenith</strong> (Zushi Zushi): stronger the higher above the ground you are</li>
<li><strong>Hyoten</strong> (Hie Hie): stronger the more enemies around you are frozen</li>
<li><strong>Netsuryo</strong> (Magu Magu): banks heat from molten ground, spent by Kagutsuchi</li>
<li><strong>Gluttony</strong> (Yami Yami): scales with the blocks you have swallowed</li>
<li><strong>White Plague</strong> (Moku Moku), <strong>Kanpa</strong> (Yuki Yuki), <strong>Shared Fate</strong> (Wara Wara), <strong>Sunless Brand</strong> (Kage Kage): each extends its fruit's own loop</li>
</ul>
<h4>Quality of Life</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Animations</strong>: charged abilities now play a wind-up pose, so an attack can be seen coming</li>
<li><strong>Sounds</strong>: new sound effects across the Moku, Yuki, Gasu, Suna, Hie and Magu kits</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3>Fixes</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ability descriptions</strong>: 18 abilities showed a raw translation key instead of their description, and 46 more had text that no longer matched what the ability did</li>
<li><strong>Performance</strong>: fixed a lag source that ran on every block face on every chunk rebuild, caused by the invisible block system</li>
<li><strong>Icons</strong>: reworked the Suke Suke no Mi icons, and redrawn Kousoku</li>
</ul>
<hr>

<ul>
<li>Minecraft <strong>1.20.1</strong></li>
<li>Forge <strong>47.4.18+</strong></li>
<li><strong>Mine Mine no Mi</strong> <code>1.20.1-0.11.5</code></li>
<li><strong>AkumaLib</strong> <code>1.0.0</code> — <strong>new in this version</strong></li>
<li><em>(Recommended)</em> <strong>Mine Mine no Mi: Awaken Path</strong> for survival progression</li>
</ul>
