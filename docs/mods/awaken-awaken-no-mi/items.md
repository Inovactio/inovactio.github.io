# Items

## BakuMetal Ingot

Made by the **Baku Baku no Mi** awakening: [Bakumetal Genesis](fruits/baku-baku-no-mi.md#bakumetal-genesis) fuses **1 Obsidian, 1 Diamond, 1 Gold Ingot and 1 Iron Ingot** from the user's inventory into one ingot. It is the material of the whole BakuMetal set, and repairs it.

## Candy

A creature that dies while under [Candy Transmutation](effects.md), or killed by the [Candy Transmutation](fruits/pero-pero-no-mi.md#candy-transmutation) projectile, drops a **Candy**. It restores **4 hunger** (0.3 saturation), can be eaten even when full, and is eaten quickly.
