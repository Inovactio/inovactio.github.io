# Navigator

The Navigator learns by **discovering places**: every chunk they have never been in, and every new land and new kind of place in their Log Book. At their bench, the **Chart Table**, they make the tools that find the way: the Log Pose, the Barometer, the Sea Chart and the Eternal Pose. At the helm, they make the Carpenter's boats go a little faster.

## Exploring

A Navigator earns **2 XP for every chunk they have never been in**, on foot, by boat or in the air, in any dimension. Going back over old ground pays nothing.

## The Log Book

Open it with the **Log Book** button on the Navigator's page of the professions book. It has two sections, filled for any player in the Overworld:

- **Lands**: every Overworld biome you stand in (+25 Navigator XP for each new one).
- **Places**: every kind of structure you stand in or come within 8 blocks of, from vanilla, Mine Mine no Mi and other mods, except buried treasure (+50 Navigator XP for each new one).

Each tenth of a section you complete pays Belly to anyone, and XP to a Navigator: from 500 Belly and 50 XP at 10 % up to 100 000 Belly and 6 000 XP at 100 %. See [Bestiary and Log Book](../bestiary-and-logbook.md).

## Level 1: the Log Pose

![](../icons/log_pose.png){ .item-icon }The **Log Pose** (does not stack) is a needle under glass that points, like a compass, to the **nearest biome of a chosen kind**.

- **Use** it to switch to the next kind of land your Navigator level opens: it searches and points to the nearest one ("Log Pose set on Forests: N blocks").
- Use it **crouching** to search again for the same kind from where you stand.
- It searches **2 400 blocks** around (**4 800** from Navigator level 25), with a 3-second cooldown.
- Only a Navigator can set it; anyone can follow the needle.

| Kind of land | Navigator level |
|---|---|
| Forests, Beaches, Plains | 1 |
| Deserts, Savannas, Taigas | 10 |
| Jungles, Swamps, Badlands | 20 |
| Mountains, Snowy lands | 30 |
| Dark Forests, Cherry Groves | 40 |
| Mushroom Fields | 60 |
| Ice Spikes | 80 |
| the Deep Dark (searched underground) | 100 |

Deserts, Swamps, Plains, Snowy lands and Mushroom Fields also include other mods' biomes of the same kind.

## Level 20: the Barometer

![](../icons/barometer.png){ .item-icon }The **Barometer** (does not stack) tells anyone who uses it what the weather will do, in real minutes:

- "Fair weather, set for about N minutes"
- "Clear for now - rain in about N minutes"
- "Raining - it clears in about N minutes"
- "Raining - a storm breaks in about N minutes"
- "Storm - it passes in about N minutes"

Where there is no sky (the Nether, the End) the needle does not move. It only forecasts the weather; it does not change it.

## Level 30: the Sea Chart

![](../icons/sea_chart.png){ .item-icon }The **Sea Chart** (stacks to 16) is a blank chart.

- **Use** it to pick the next kind of place your level opens; use it **crouching** to draw it.
- It becomes a map ("Sea Chart: Villages") with the nearest such place marked: ocean monuments and woodland mansions with their vanilla icons, the rest with an X. It searches up to 1 600 blocks around.
- Like a cartographer's map, a chart never leads to a place already charted in the world.
- Drawing uses one blank chart, gives **20 Navigator XP**, and has a 3-second cooldown.
- Only a Navigator can draw one; anyone can read, sell or give the drawn map.

| Kind of place | Navigator level | What it finds |
|---|---|---|
| Villages, Shipwrecks, Ocean Ruins, Pirate Ships, Training Grounds | 30 | villages, shipwrecks, ocean ruins; Mine Mine no Mi's small and medium pirate ships, its ghost ship and its training structures |
| Marine Posts, Bandit Hideouts, Pillager Outposts, Temples | 40 | Mine Mine no Mi's small Marine bases, watch towers and bandit structures; pillager outposts; desert and jungle pyramids |
| Ruined Portals, Witch Huts, Igloos, Trail Ruins | 50 | the vanilla structures |
| Marine Fortresses, Warships, Ocean Monuments | 60 | Mine Mine no Mi's large Marine base and large ships; ocean monuments |
| Sky Islands | 70 | Mine Mine no Mi's sky islands |
| Woodland Mansions | 80 | the vanilla structure |
| Ancient Cities | 90 | the vanilla structure |
| Strongholds | 100 | the vanilla structure |

## Level 50: the Eternal Pose

![](../icons/eternal_pose.png){ .item-icon }The **Eternal Pose** (stacks to 16, uncommon) points to one place, for good.

- A Navigator uses a blank one to **calibrate it where they stand** (it takes one from the stack; **+40 Navigator XP**). From then on it points there and cannot be set again.
- It is named after the place: the structure they stood in (within 8 blocks), otherwise the biome, or whatever name an anvil gave it.
- Anyone can use a calibrated one to see how far away the place is ("It points to X: N blocks away", or "in another world").

## At the helm

From level 10, the Carpenter's boats you steer go 1 % faster, on any water; from level 40, 2 % faster (replacing the 1 %). The boat's total speed boost (hull, shape, sail and helmsman together) is capped at ×1.075, so a Navigator makes an ordinary boat faster but adds little to the fastest one. See [Boats](../boats.md) and the [Carpenter](carpenter.md).

## Advancements

The **Navigation** tab opens at Navigator level 1: **Navigation** (become a Navigator), **Apprentice Navigator** (level 5), **Which Way?** (make a Log Pose), **Storm Warning** (make a Barometer), **Charting the Seas** (make a Sea Chart), **A Way Home** (make an Eternal Pose), **The Whole World** (every land in the Log Book) and **Every Port of Call** (every kind of place in the Log Book).
