# Navigator

The Navigator learns by **discovering places**: every chunk they have never been in, and every new land and new kind of place in their Log Book. At their bench, the **Chart Table**, they make the tools that find the way: the Log Pose, the Sounding Lead, the Barometer, the Sea Chart and the Eternal Pose. At the helm, they make the Carpenter's boats go a little faster.

## Exploring

A Navigator earns **2 XP for every chunk they have never been in**, on foot, by boat or in the air, in any dimension. Going back over old ground pays nothing.

## The Log Book

Open it with the **Log Book** button on the Navigator's page of the professions book. It has two sections, filled for any player in the Overworld:

- **Lands**: every Overworld biome you stand in (+25 Navigator XP for each new one).
- **Places**: every kind of structure you stand in or come within 8 blocks of, from vanilla, Mine Mine no Mi and other mods, except buried treasure (+50 Navigator XP for each new one).

The book also keeps the **names of the [villages](../villages.md)** you have been to, and lists a [town](../towns.md) as one. Minecraft's own villages no longer generate, so they are no longer among the places to find.

Each tenth of a section you complete pays Belly to anyone, and XP to a Navigator: from 500 Belly and 50 XP at 10 % up to 100 000 Belly and 6 000 XP at 100 %. See [Bestiary and Log Book](../bestiary-and-logbook.md).

## Level 1: the Log Pose

![](../icons/log_pose.png){ .item-icon }The **Log Pose** (does not stack) is a needle under glass that points, like a compass, to the **nearest biome of a chosen kind**.

- **Use** it to switch to the next kind of land your Navigator level opens: it searches and points to the nearest one ("Log Pose set on Forests: N blocks").
- Use it **crouching** to search again for the same kind from where you stand.
- It searches **2 400 blocks** around (**4 800** with the Long Sight talent, 10 % more for each Keen eye small talent), with a 3-second cooldown.
- Only a Navigator can set it; anyone can follow the needle.

| Kind of land | Navigator level |
|---|---|
| Forests, Beaches, Plains | 1 |
| Deserts, Savannas, Taigas | 10 |
| Jungles, Swamps, Badlands | 20 |
| Mountains, Snowy lands | 30 |
| Dark Forests, Cherry Groves | 40 |
| Mushroom Fields | 60 |
| Ice Spikes | 80 |
| the Deep Dark (searched underground) | 100 |

Deserts, Swamps, Plains, Snowy lands and Mushroom Fields also include other mods' biomes of the same kind.

## Level 10: the Sounding Lead

The **Sounding Lead** is a lead on its line, made at the Chart Table from an Iron Ingot and three String.

- **Use** it over water, from the shore or from a boat, and it tells you how deep the water is: "The lead sounds N blocks deep".
- It sounds the water you look at; failing that, the water under you or under your boat.
- Fish bite by depth, and the lead counts it as the fishing does: the crew's [Fisher](fisher.md) will want one.
- It reads the depth of Sky Island's sea of clouds too, but not lava.
- Only a Navigator makes it; **anyone can use it**.

## Level 20: the Barometer

![](../icons/barometer.png){ .item-icon }The **Barometer** (does not stack) tells anyone who uses it what the weather will do, in real minutes:

- "Fair weather, set for about N minutes"
- "Clear for now - rain in about N minutes"
- "Raining - it clears in about N minutes"
- "Raining - a storm breaks in about N minutes"
- "Storm - it passes in about N minutes"

Where there is no sky (the Nether, the End) the needle does not move. It only forecasts the weather; it does not change it.

It also foretells a **storm at sea**. Half a day before one breaks, the Barometer says which way it is brewing, about how far, and in how many minutes it breaks: the Navigator knows before anyone. While it rages, the Barometer says when it will pass:

- "A storm is brewing at sea to the *west*, about N blocks away - it breaks in about N minutes"
- "A storm rages at sea to the *west*, about N blocks away - it passes in about N minutes"
- "You are in the storm - it passes in about N minutes"

See [Events at sea](../events-at-sea.md#a-storm-at-sea) for the storm itself.

## Level 30: the Sea Chart

![](../icons/sea_chart.png){ .item-icon }The **Sea Chart** (stacks to 16) is a blank chart.

- **Use** it to pick the next kind of place your level opens; use it **crouching** to draw it.
- It becomes a map ("Sea Chart: Villages") with the nearest such place marked: ocean monuments and woodland mansions with their vanilla icons, the rest with an X. It searches up to 1 600 blocks around.
- Like a cartographer's map, a chart never leads to a place already charted in the world.
- The **Villages** kind leads to Cruise's own [villages](../villages.md) first, the nearest not yet charted and still lived in, and to one of Minecraft's villages when there is none in reach (in a world that still has them). A chart drawn to a [town](../towns.md) is named for it: "Sea Chart: Town". The chart takes a few seconds to draw; the game does not stop meanwhile.
- Drawing uses one blank chart, gives **20 Navigator XP**, and has a 3-second cooldown.
- Only a Navigator can draw one; anyone can read, sell or give the drawn map.

| Kind of place | Navigator level | What it finds |
|---|---|---|
| Villages, Shipwrecks, Ocean Ruins, Pirate Ships, Training Grounds | 30 | villages, shipwrecks, ocean ruins; Mine Mine no Mi's small and medium pirate ships, its ghost ship and its training structures |
| Marine Posts, Bandit Hideouts, Pillager Outposts, Temples | 40 | Mine Mine no Mi's small Marine bases, watch towers and bandit structures; pillager outposts; desert and jungle pyramids |
| Ruined Portals, Witch Huts, Igloos, Trail Ruins | 50 | the vanilla structures |
| Marine Fortresses, Warships, Ocean Monuments | 60 | Mine Mine no Mi's large Marine base and large ships; ocean monuments |
| Sky Islands | 70 | Mine Mine no Mi's sky islands |
| Woodland Mansions | 80 | the vanilla structure |
| Ancient Cities | 90 | the vanilla structure |
| Strongholds | 100 | the vanilla structure |

## Level 50: the Eternal Pose

![](../icons/eternal_pose.png){ .item-icon }The **Eternal Pose** (stacks to 16, uncommon) points to one place, for good.

- A Navigator uses a blank one to **calibrate it where they stand** (it takes one from the stack; **+40 Navigator XP**). From then on it points there and cannot be set again.
- It is named after the place: the structure they stood in (within 8 blocks), otherwise the biome, or whatever name an anvil gave it.
- Anyone can use a calibrated one to see how far away the place is ("It points to X: N blocks away", or "in another world").

## At the helm

With the Fair Wind talent, the Carpenter's boats you steer go 1 % faster, on any water; with Trade Winds too, 2 % faster. The boat's total speed boost (hull, shape, sail and helmsman together) is capped at ×1.075, so a Navigator makes an ordinary boat faster but adds little to the fastest one. See [Boats](../boats.md) and the [Carpenter](carpenter.md).

## Talents

Every 5 Navigator levels give you a **talent point** (10 at level 50, 20 at level 100). Spend them in the Navigator's web of talents, opened by the **Talents** button on the trade's page of the trades book or with the **Talents** key (**J** by default). Each talent costs one point. You can take a talent that starts the web, or one linked to a talent you already have. Small talents of one kind add up. A point stays spent until a [Potion of Second Thoughts](chemist.md#the-potion-of-second-thoughts) gives them back. See [Talents](../trades-and-levels.md#talents).

### Combat abilities

Taking an ability talent puts its ability in the **Profession** tab of the abilities screen, and you place it on your bar yourself. It costs nothing from your bag. Its small "Fighting Navigator" talents make the Navigator's abilities hit harder. Damage is on the base mod's scale, as in the ability screen.

| Ability | What it does | Needs | Cooldown | Damage | Range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Thunder Call** | Call lightning on the enemy you look at within 16 blocks. It starts no fire | a Barometer in your bag | 15 s | 30 | 16 blocks |
| **Gust** | Blow a gust before you. Every enemy in front of you within 8 blocks is hit and blown far back | a Barometer in your bag | 10 s | 20 | 8 blocks |
| **Mirage** | Vanish for 5 seconds. The creatures after you lose you | a Barometer in your bag | 30 s | — | — |

## Advancements

The **Navigation** tab opens at Navigator level 1: **Navigation** (become a Navigator), **Apprentice Navigator** (level 5), **Which Way?** (make a Log Pose), **By the Mark** (make a Sounding Lead), **Storm Warning** (make a Barometer), **Charting the Seas** (make a Sea Chart), **A Way Home** (make an Eternal Pose), **The Whole World** (every land in the Log Book) and **Every Port of Call** (every kind of place in the Log Book).
