# Merchant

The Merchant makes nothing and gathers nothing: he **sells**, and learns by selling. Every Belly he earns from Cruise's travelling merchants, from their contracts and from the Auction House counts toward his level, and only he can read the torn treasure maps that turn up in chests, on stalls and on the end of a fishing line.

| | |
|---|---|
| Workstation | none: the travelling merchants and the [Auction House](../auction-house.md) |
| Advancements tab | Trade |
| Levels | 1 to 100; torn maps up to level 75 |

You only earn Merchant XP if you practise the Merchant trade (in Crew mode, if it is your trade). Someone of another trade can still sell, deliver contracts and use the Auction House, but gets none of the Merchant's XP, prices or bonuses, and cannot read a torn map.

## How a Merchant earns XP

| Source | XP |
|---|---|
| Selling goods to a Cruise merchant (the Sell window) | 1 XP per 10 Belly of the sale (at least 1) |
| Delivering a merchant's contract | 1 XP per 10 Belly of the reward (at least 1) |
| One of your lots selling at the Auction House | 1 XP per 10 Belly of its price (at least 1); if you are offline, it waits and is given when you log in or collect |
| Being the first to open a buried treasure chest | Rumor map 100 XP, Old map 400 XP, Legendary map 1 200 XP |

## Selling to the travelling merchants

Five travelling merchants visit the Overworld: the **Fishmonger**, the **Hunting Merchant**, the **Travelling Cook**, the **Prospector** and the **Materials Trader**. Each buys the goods of one trade through a Sell window, keeps a stall, and brings three contracts.

From level 1, everything you sell to them pays **+0.5 % per level**, up to **+25 % at level 50**; the bonus stops growing there. At level 10 the market fills up **half as fast** with what you sell, at level 25 you pay **15 % less** for what you buy from them, and at level 40 a merchant's purse **no longer limits** what he buys from you (your own Belly cap still does).

Who comes where and when, what each one buys and for how much: see [Merchants and contracts](../merchants-and-contracts.md).

## Contracts

Every merchant arrives with three contracts for goods of his own trade, paid well above what he would pay over the counter, and not out of his purse. Your price bonus raises the reward too.

From **level 10**, merchants also show you **the third contract**, the one they keep for Merchants: it always pays the top premium, and half the time adds a Torn Old Map. See [Merchants and contracts](../merchants-and-contracts.md).

## The Auction House

The [Auction House](../auction-house.md) is one market for the whole server, where players list lots for each other. A Merchant has it easier, and more so with every level:

- the **listing fee** falls by 1.5 % of itself per level, down to a quarter of the normal fee at level 50 (2 % becomes 0.5 % with the default fee);
- **+1 lot** you may have up every 5 levels;
- **+1 day** a lot stays up every 2 levels;
- from level 1, the **price history**: what a kind of good has sold for lately, on average.

The fee stops falling at 50; the extra lots and days keep growing up to level 100.

## Treasure maps

Only a Merchant can decipher a torn treasure map: a **Torn Rumor Map** from level 1, a **Torn Old Map** from level 20, a **Torn Legendary Map** from level 75. The map then points to a buried chest, and the first to open it earns the grade's Merchant XP. See [Treasure hunts](../treasure-hunts.md).

## Level perks

| Level | Perk | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1+ | "Sales and contracts with Cruise's merchants: +…%" | +0.5 % per level on everything you sell to a Cruise merchant and on every contract reward, up to +25 % at level 50 (no more after that) |
| 1+ | "Auction House: fee -…%, +… lots up, +… days up, the prices' history" | lower listing fee (down to a quarter at 50), +1 lot every 5 levels, +1 day every 2 levels, and the average price lately of each kind of good |
| 10 | "The market fills half as fast with what you sell" | you can sell twice as many of one kind (32 instead of 16) before its price halves |
| 10 | "Merchants show you the contract they keep for Merchants" | you see, and can deliver, each merchant's third contract |
| 25 | "15% off Cruise merchants' stalls" | 15 % off everything you buy from a Cruise merchant; the Buy screen still shows the full price, but you are charged less. Not on Mine Mine no Mi's own traders |
| 40 | "A merchant's purse no longer limits what he buys from you" | a merchant buys everything you bring, whatever is left in his purse |

The professions book shows the two level-1 perks with their **current** values, so they grow as you level.

## Advancements

The **Trade** tab opens at Merchant level 1: **Trade** (become a Merchant), **Apprentice Merchant** (level 5), **A Deal Is a Deal** (deliver a merchant's contract), **Going Once, Going Twice** (sell a lot at the Auction House), **X Marks the Spot** (dig up a treasure) and **Legendary Treasure** (dig up the treasure of a legendary map).
