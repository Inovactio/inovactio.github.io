# Merchant

The Merchant makes nothing and gathers nothing: they **sell**, and learn by selling. Every Belly they earn from Cruise's travelling merchants, from their contracts and at the Auction House counts toward their level. They are also the only ones who can read the torn treasure maps that turn up in chests, on stalls and on the end of a fishing line.

| | |
|---|---|
| Workstation | none: the travelling merchants and the [Auction House](../auction-house.md) |
| Advancements tab | Trade |
| Levels | 1 to 100; torn maps up to level 75 |

You only earn Merchant XP if you practise the Merchant trade (in Crew mode, if it is your trade). A player who does not can still sell, deliver contracts and use the Auction House, but gets none of the Merchant's XP, prices or bonuses, and cannot read a torn map.

## How a Merchant earns XP

| Source | XP |
|---|---|
| Selling goods to a Cruise merchant (the Sell window) | 1 XP per 10 Belly of the sale (at least 1) |
| Delivering a merchant's contract | 1 XP per 10 Belly of the reward (at least 1) |
| One of your lots selling at the Auction House | 1 XP per 10 Belly of its price (at least 1); if you are offline, it is given when you log in or collect |
| Fetching the takings of your stall in a town's market hall | 1 XP per 10 Belly fetched, as a sale gives |
| Being the first to open a buried treasure chest | Rumor map 100 XP, Old map 400 XP, Legendary map 1 200 XP |

## Selling to the travelling merchants

Five travelling merchants visit the Overworld: the **Fishmonger**, the **Hunting Merchant**, the **Travelling Cook**, the **Prospector** and the **Materials Trader**. Each buys the goods of one trade through a Sell window, keeps a stall and brings three contracts.

A Merchant sells to them on better terms:

- from level 1, everything you sell pays **+0.5 % per level**, up to **+25 % at level 50**, where the bonus stops growing;
- with the **Trade Network** talent, the market fills up **half as fast** with what you sell (slower again with Wide Network and the Contacts talents);
- with **Bargain**, you pay **15 % less** for what you buy from them (a village cured of its [fever](../village-events.md#a-fever-in-the-village) gives 20 % to those who helped, and a village under your group's [flag](../village-flags.md) 10 %: you keep the best of them, never more than one);
- with **Credit**, a merchant's purse **no longer limits** what he buys from you (your own Belly cap still does).

Who comes where and when, and what each one buys and for how much: see [Merchants and contracts](../merchants-and-contracts.md).

## A stall in a town's market hall

In a [town](../towns.md)'s market hall the five merchants are always there, each at his own stall, with a purse twice as heavy and five contracts instead of three. And a Merchant can **rent a stall** of their own there.

- **Renting.** Speak to the hall's **clerk**, by the way in: a stall costs **2 000 Belly a week**, paid ahead. One stall a town, in as many towns as you like. Only a Merchant can rent.
- **Stocking.** Lay on its **27 squares** what a Cruise merchant buys: fish, creatures, minerals, materials, dishes.
- **Selling.** The town's people buy off it every day, whether you are there or not: **8 pieces a day, and one more every 5 Merchant levels** (18 a day at level 50, where it stops growing). Each piece sells for **half as much again** as its merchant gives, with your own price bonus on top (up to ×1.875 at level 50). A day's sale is made when the day ends.
- **The takings.** Come back to the clerk to fetch them. They pay Merchant XP as any sale does.
- **The lease.** You can pay a week more once a week or less is left. A stall you do not pay for again is given back: what is still on it and what it took in wait for you with the clerk.
- A town that is closed to you closes your stall for as long: nothing sells, nothing is lost, and the rent runs on.

Two goods that Mine Mine no Mi's own traders sell, Lapis Lazuli and Sea King Meat, never fetch more on a stall than those traders' lowest price.

## Contracts

Every merchant arrives with three contracts for goods of his own trade (five in a town's market hall, new each day). They pay 1.5 to 1.8 times what he would pay over the counter, not out of his purse, and your price bonus raises the reward too.

With the **Trade Network** talent, merchants also show you **the last contract**, the one they keep for Merchants: it always pays the top premium (×1.8), and half the time adds a Torn Old Map. See [Merchants and contracts](../merchants-and-contracts.md).

## The Auction House

The [Auction House](../auction-house.md) is one market for the whole server, where players list lots for each other. A Merchant has it easier with every level:

- the **listing fee** falls by 1.5 % of itself per level, down to a quarter of the normal fee at level 50 (2 % becomes 0.5 % with the default fee);
- **+1 lot** you may have up every 5 levels;
- **+1 day** a lot stays up every 2 levels;
- from level 1, the **price history**: what a kind of good has sold for lately, on average.

The fee stops falling at level 50; the extra lots and days keep growing up to level 100.

## Treasure maps

Only a Merchant can decipher a torn treasure map: a **Torn Rumor Map** from level 1, a **Torn Old Map** from level 20, a **Torn Legendary Map** from level 75. The map then leads to a buried chest, and the first player to open it earns the grade's Merchant XP. See [Treasure hunts](../treasure-hunts.md).

## What grows with your level

Two bonuses are no talents: they grow with your Merchant level, and the trades book shows them with their **current** values.

| From level | Bonus | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Sales and contracts with Cruise's merchants: +…%" | +0.5 % per level on everything you sell to a Cruise merchant and on every contract reward, up to +25 % at level 50 (no more after that) |
| 1 | "Auction House: fee -…%, +… lots up, +… days up, the prices' history" | a lower listing fee (down to a quarter at level 50), +1 lot every 5 levels, +1 day every 2 levels, and the average recent price of each kind of good |

## Talents

Every 5 Merchant levels give you a **talent point** (10 at level 50, 20 at level 100). Spend them in the Merchant's web of talents, opened by the **Talents** button on the trade's page of the trades book or with the **Talents** key (**J** by default). Each talent costs one point. You can take a talent that starts the web, or one linked to a talent you already have. Small talents of one kind add up. A point stays spent until a [Potion of Second Thoughts](chemist.md#the-potion-of-second-thoughts) gives them back. See [Talents](../trades-and-levels.md#talents).

### Combat abilities

Taking an ability talent puts its ability in the **Profession** tab of the abilities screen, and you place it on your bar yourself. It costs nothing from your bag. Its small "Fighting Merchant" talents make the Merchant's abilities hit harder. Damage is on the base mod's scale, as in the ability screen.

| Ability | What it does | Needs | Cooldown | Damage | Range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Golden Bulwark** | A shield of gold of 6 health for you and your crew within 8 blocks | — | 45 s | — | 8 blocks |
| **Coin Toss** | Throw a fistful of coins at the enemy you look at within 14 blocks. They cost you nothing | — | 8 s | 25 | 14 blocks |
| **Bribe** | Pay off the enemy you look at within 8 blocks. For 10 seconds it stops fighting you and will not pick you again. Not a player or a boss | — | 20 s | — | 8 blocks |

## Advancements

The **Trade** tab opens at Merchant level 1: **Trade** (become a Merchant), **Apprentice Merchant** (level 5), **A Deal Is a Deal** (deliver a merchant's contract), **Going Once, Going Twice** (sell a lot at the Auction House), **X Marks the Spot** (dig up a treasure) and **Legendary Treasure** (dig up the treasure of a legendary map).
