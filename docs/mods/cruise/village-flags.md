# A village under your flag

A [village](villages.md) or a [town](towns.md) has a hall and a flag: a pirate crew, the Marines or the Revolutionaries can put it under theirs. It is won by trust, by fear or by force, it pays a rent, it is raided, and on a server another group of players can take it from you.

!!! note "Villages with a hall only"
    Only a village made since 0.4.0 has a hall and someone at its head. A village made before has neither, and never passes under a flag.

Everything is asked at the hall, of **the one at the village's head**: a mayor in the plains, a governor in the desert, a chief in the snowy plains, an elder in the savanna, a jarl in the taiga. Right click him: he says where the village stands with you, and offers what you may do.

There is no limit to the villages a group holds. Everything on this page but the [assaults between players](#assaults-between-players) works in a single-player world too.

## Who can hold a village

| Group | Who takes a village, or gives it back | Who collects the coffer |
|---|---|---|
| **A pirate crew** | its captain alone | its captain |
| **The Marines** | any Marine player | whoever raised the flag |
| **The Revolutionaries** | any Revolutionary player | whoever raised the flag |

- A pirate without a crew, a Bounty Hunter or a player of no side holds no village.
- If the Marine or the Revolutionary who raised the flag leaves his side, the village stays his side's: the first comrade who speaks to its head answers for it from then on.
- A crew that is no more loses its villages.
- A village that is closed to you because of a crime will not take your flag.

Which way is open depends on who holds the village now:

| The village is... | How it is taken |
|---|---|
| nobody's (a bare mast) | [by trust](#by-trust), or [by fear](#by-fear) |
| the Marines', a pirate crew's or the Revolutionaries' | [by force](#by-force) |
| another group of players' | by an [assault between players](#assaults-between-players) |

## By trust

A village takes the flag of those it trusts: **twenty good turns** done to it. For a crew, every member's good turns count together.

A good turn is:

- an order delivered to its [notice board](villages.md#the-notice-board);
- a help during its fever (see [Village events](village-events.md));
- in a town, an order in bulk filled at its [warehouse](towns.md#the-warehouse).

When you have twenty, ask at the hall to raise your flag. No Belly is asked.

## By fear

A **pirate captain** may threaten the one at the village's head instead. He gives way to a bounty of **10,000 Belly for each villager**: a village of twenty people gives way to a bounty of 200,000 Belly. He says the sum, and yours.

It is quick and it is poor: see the table below. A village that fears you slips away in days unless you come back to threaten, or earn its trust. Good turns still count: at twenty, its head offers to pass to trust, and the village is then fully loyal.

## Trust and fear compared

| | By trust | By fear |
|---|---|---|
| Who | a pirate crew, the Marines, the Revolutionaries | a pirate crew only |
| What it takes | twenty good turns | a bounty of 10,000 Belly for each villager |
| Loyalty at the start | 100 of 100 | 50 of 100 |
| Rent, at full loyalty | 40 Belly a day for each villager | 20 Belly a day for each villager |
| Share of what its stalls take in | 5 % | none |
| Price at its stalls for your group | 10 % off | no discount |
| Its people | as with anyone | step away from you |
| Loyalty over time | does not wear away | loses 10 a day |
| A raid lost | costs 30 of its loyalty | the village is lost at once |
| A town's guards, in a raid or an assault | fight beside you | keep to their posts |

The 10 % off is the friend's price. Only the best discount counts: it is never added to another.

## Loyalty

A village's loyalty goes from 0 to 100. **It sets the rent**: a village half loyal pays half. **At nothing the flag comes down**, and the village is nobody's again.

| What happens | Loyalty |
|---|---|
| One of your group strikes a villager, takes a boat, or breaks its blocks once too often | 5 less |
| One of your group kills a villager | 20 less |
| One of your group does a good turn to a village that trusts you | 5 more |
| A raid repelled, or an assault of players repelled, in a village that trusts you | 10 more |
| A raid lost, in a village that trusts you | 30 less |
| A day goes by, in a village that fears you | 10 less |
| You threaten its head again, in a village that fears you | back to 50 |

- A village that fears you is not made more loyal by good turns or by a raid repelled: good turns earn its trust, which is another matter.
- **Threatening again** is offered once the village's fear has worn under 50, once a day at most, and only while your bounty still frightens it.

## The coffer

The rent is kept in the hall's coffer.

- **Each day** the village puts in its rent: 40 Belly for each living villager under trust, 20 under fear, and of that the share its loyalty gives. A villager killed and not yet replaced pays nothing.
- **A village that trusts you** adds 5 % of what players spend at its stalls, and in a town of a night's price at its inn.
- **A raid repelled** by a village that trusts you is thanked: 100 Belly for each villager.

Collect it at the hall: the crew's captain, or for the Marines and the Revolutionaries whoever raised the flag. Striking your own flag hands you what the coffer holds.

**Your flag flies on the hall's mast** (for a crew, its own Jolly Roger), and your group is told what becomes of its villages.

## Raids

Every **twenty to thirty days** a village you hold is raided by your enemies.

| You are... | The raiders are... |
|---|---|
| pirates or Revolutionaries | the Marines |
| Marines | pirates |

- **You are told a day ahead**, and the village's head says so too.
- **It comes at dusk**, and only while one of your group is in the world. Otherwise it is put off.
- The raiders land at the village's edge, as many as the village is large, and make for the square. In a town they come in by its gates.
- **Beat every one of them within five minutes.**
- A bar at the top of the screen shows your group how many are left and how much time. The last three are seen through walls.
- The villagers stay in and cannot be hurt. What is broken [mends](villages.md#villages-mend-themselves).
- In a town that trusts you the guards fight beside you; in one that fears you they keep to their posts.

| The raid is... | A village that trusts you | A village that fears you |
|---|---|---|
| repelled | 10 more loyalty, and 100 Belly for each villager into the coffer | as it was |
| lost | 30 less loyalty | lost at once: the flag comes down |

A Peaceful world has no raids.

## By force

A village that someone keeps is not won by good turns or by a great bounty: it is taken from them.

| The village is... | Their place | Who can take it |
|---|---|---|
| **the Marines'** | their post among the houses, or the large base beside the village | a pirate crew, the Revolutionaries |
| **a pirate crew's** | their den among the houses, or their ship moored off the port | another crew, the Revolutionaries, the Marines |
| **the Revolutionaries'** | their camp in the wilds near the village | a pirate crew, the Marines |

Three steps:

1. **Empty their place** of its men: the post or the base, the den or the ship, the camp.
2. **Declare your assault** at the hall. For a crew, its captain alone.
3. **Beat their reinforcements within five minutes.** They land at once, as many as the village is large, a captain among them. Where the Marines' large base stands they are twice as many, with a vice admiral at their head.

The assault follows a raid's rules: the same bar, the last ones seen through walls, the villagers under cover. In a town the reinforcements come in by its gates, and the guards stay out of it. A Peaceful world has no assault.

**Win, and the village is yours.**

| Taken by | What the village becomes |
|---|---|
| A pirate crew | ruled by fear |
| The Revolutionaries | freed: it trusts them half way (loyalty 50 of 100) |
| The Marines | freed the same way |

- The first raid on a village taken by force comes sooner: five to ten days after it is taken.
- If your flag falls there, those you took it from are back in their place, and the village is theirs again.

**Fail, and they settle in again.**

- Your group waits **three days** before another try on that village.
- Where the village is the Marines', each of your group who was there has a bounty put on his head: 5,000 Belly more, or 10,000 where the large base stands. Pirates and Revolutionaries put no price on anyone.

## Assaults between players

On a server, a village another group of players holds can be taken from them. Where players cannot fight one another, there are no assaults.

**Declaring.** Declare your assault at the village's hall; for a crew, its captain alone. Both groups are told, each holder is told again as he comes into the world, and the village's head says so to whoever asks.

- A group has **one assault declared at a time**, and a village one assault at a time.
- No assault is declared on a village while it is being raided, and no raid comes to a village while an assault is declared on it.
- **From the declaration to the end the coffer is sealed and the flag cannot be struck**: both are what is fought for.
- You may **call it off** at the hall before it begins, at the price of three days' wait.

**When.** It takes place **at dusk, a whole day later at least**, and only if one of the holders is in the world then. Otherwise it is put off to the next dusk. After **seven such dusks** the village falls without a fight.

**The fight.**

| | |
|---|---|
| How long | five minutes at most |
| To win | hold the village's square **sixty seconds in a row** |
| Holding the square | one of you on its pavement, none of them. The roofs of its well and of its stalls do not count |
| A holder steps back onto it | the count starts again from nothing |
| Whoever is killed | is out of it until it ends |
| Every assailant down | the assault is repelled at once |

A bar at the top of the screen shows both groups the count.

**Holders who are fewer** than their assailants are joined by fighters of their own side (Marines, pirates or Revolutionaries): two for each player they lack, twelve at most. They strike the assailants and nobody else, and do not count for the square. A town that trusts its holders has its guards fight the assailants too.

**Afterwards.**

| | What happens |
|---|---|
| The assailants win | the village is theirs, and its coffer with it: ruled by fear under a crew, trusting half way under the Marines or the Revolutionaries |
| The holders win | the village is the more loyal for it, as after a raid repelled, and the assailants wait **three days** before another try |
| Either way | the village is spared any assault for **five days** |

The beaten lose the flag, and nothing else.
