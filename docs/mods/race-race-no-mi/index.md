# Race Race no Mi

![](../../assets/icons/race-race-no-mi.png){ .mod-icon }

**Race Race no Mi** brings the races of One Piece to [Mine Mine no Mi](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-mod). You pick your race and sub-race in the character creator: each one has its own body, its own passive and, for every race but the humans and the fishmen, techniques of their own in the abilities tree.

## Your size

Every race has a size, set in the character creator with a slider: a man is 1, a fishman 1.25, a Lunarian 1.5, an Oni 1.75, a giant 4, a Tontatta 0.25. Reach, blows, health, strides and techniques all follow the body, through [AkumaLib](../../akumalib/index.md)'s size system: a giant's Stomp covers more ground than a man's would. The figures on these pages are for a body of size 1 unless they say otherwise (the Tontatta and Lunarian pages give them at the race's own size).

| Race | Size | Slider |

| Fishmen | 1.25 | about 0.94 to 1.56 (the same slider, times 1.25) |

## The techniques

The techniques of the sky people, the giants, the Oni, the Tontatta and the Lunarians sit in a branch of their own in the abilities tree, right of the base mod's races. Each one costs Doriki and training points, like the base mod's race techniques. A player only sees the techniques of their own race in the tree, and loses them on leaving the race (by the book or by command).
