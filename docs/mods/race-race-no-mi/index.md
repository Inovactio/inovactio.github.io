# Race Race no Mi

![](../../assets/icons/race-race-no-mi.png){ .mod-icon }

**Race Race no Mi** brings the races of One Piece to [Mine Mine no Mi](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-mod). You pick your race and sub-race in the character creator: each one has its own body, its own passive and, for every race but the humans and the fishmen, techniques of their own in the abilities tree.

## Your size

Every race has a size, set in the character creator with a slider: a man is 1, a fishman 1.25, a Lunarian 1.5, an Oni 1.75, a Buccaneer 2, a giant 4, a Tontatta 0.25. Reach, blows, health, strides and techniques all follow the body, through [AkumaLib](../../akumalib/index.md)'s size system: a giant's Stomp covers more ground than a man's would. The figures on these pages are for a body of size 1 unless they say otherwise (the Tontatta and Lunarian pages give them at the race's own size).

## The techniques

The techniques of the sky people, the giants, the Oni, the Tontatta, the Lunarians, the Buccaneers and the merfolk sit in a branch of their own in the abilities tree, right of the base mod's races. Each one costs Doriki and training points, like the base mod's race techniques. A player only sees the techniques of their own race in the tree, and loses them on leaving the race (by the book or by command). The modified humans' techniques come with the Raid Suit they wear, not from the tree.

**By command**, `/ability unlock_group` has a group for each race and sub-race (`racenomi:giant`, `racenomi:wotan`...): a race gives its whole kit, a sub-race only its own. A technique given by a command is not taken back by the race check.
