# Changelog

Every Race Race no Mi release for Minecraft 1.20.1, newest first, as published on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/race-race-no-mi/files/all).

## 0.3.0 (beta) { #v0-3-0 }

<small>Released 2026-11-06 · [Download](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/race-race-no-mi/files/TO-FILL)</small>

<div class="changelog-body" markdown="0">
<p><strong>Three new races and the Raid Suits of Germa 66.</strong> The Buccaneers, the merfolk and the modified humans join the
other races. The modified humans wear the five Raid Suits of the Vinsmoke family, picked from their ration box, each
with its own techniques. The Oni, the Tontatta and the Lunarians get two more techniques each, the giants can carry a
friend on their shoulder and climb higher, and the Tontatta jump higher, run faster and fall further unhurt.</p>
<p>Requires Minecraft <strong>1.20.1</strong>, Forge <strong>47.4.2</strong> or newer (an older Forge crashes when a chest is opened),
<strong>Mine Mine no Mi 0.11.6</strong> and <strong>AkumaLib 6.4.0</strong> or newer.
<a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-sky-island">Mine Mine no Mi: Sky Island</a> is optional.
Needed on both the client and the server. AkumaLib 6.4.0 has a new network protocol: the server and every player must
update together.</p>
<hr />
<h2>The Buccaneers (new)</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Twice a man's height</strong> (size 2, 3.6 blocks; the slider goes from 1.875 to 2.125), a broad chest and your own skin.
  Counts as a human for Rokushiki. Hold sneak to bend through a door.</li>
<li><strong>Three busts to choose from</strong> in the character creator, only a look: the <strong>Broad Buccaneer</strong> (broad shoulders and
  chest over a narrower waist), the <strong>Stout Buccaneer</strong> (belly and hips under a smaller chest) and the <strong>Great
  Buccaneer</strong> (broad all the way down).</li>
<li><strong>Buccaneer Blood</strong>: +2 bare hand damage and +2 toughness. A killing blow leaves you at 1 health instead, once every
  5 minutes.</li>
<li><strong>Six techniques</strong>:</li>
<li><strong>Living Wall</strong>: for 5 s, a blow from the front at an ally behind you lands on you instead; you take half damage
    from the front and are not pushed back. Press again to stop it early: the cooldown is then the share of the time
    used, never under a quarter.</li>
<li><strong>Crushing Hold</strong>: lifts a body up to 0.6 of your height and crushes it, 3 damage at once and each second for 4 s,
    then drops it at your feet. 8 damage on you frees it. Press again to let go early, for a shorter cooldown.</li>
<li><strong>Buccaneer Charge</strong>: a 6-block rush shoulder first, every enemy on the way takes 7 and is thrown off the path.
    <strong>ALT Buccaneer Palm</strong>: an open palm ahead, 10 damage, throws the enemy far back, no run needed.</li>
<li><strong>Ground Breaker</strong>: after a 1 s cast, both fists down: 12 damage to every enemy within 5 blocks, thrown up.</li>
<li><strong>Rock Hurl</strong>: tears a rock from the ground and throws it where you look; it bursts for 9 within 2.5 blocks. The
    hole closes after 20 s.</li>
<li><strong>Answering Fist</strong>: punches the nearest enemy in front of you for 10 and throws it far, up to twice as hard the more
    health you lost in the last 5 s (twice at half your health).</li>
<li><strong>Every technique has its own icon, pose and effects</strong>, and each bust its own face in the character creator's book.</li>
</ul>
<h2>The Merfolk (new)</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>A fish tail in the water, legs on land.</strong> The tail comes out when you swim off the bottom, and your leggings and
  boots are hidden while it is out.</li>
<li><strong>Two sub-races</strong>: the <strong>Mermaid</strong> (a man's size, coral tail, <strong>Swift Tail</strong>: +50 % swim speed) and the <strong>Great
  Merfolk</strong> (size 1.25, grey-blue tail, <strong>Great Body</strong>: +2 toughness).</li>
<li><strong>Merfolk Body</strong>: swim 4.5 times as fast (a fishman 3 times), breathe and see in the dark under water. With a Devil
  Fruit you lose all of it, unless a Bubbly Coral keeps you dry.</li>
<li><strong>Fishman Karate</strong> at a fishman's price for the five techniques open to every race. Two Fish Engine and Yarinami stay
  the fishmen's.</li>
<li><strong>Six techniques</strong>, each with its own icon, pose and water effects (the trident is drawn as one):</li>
<li><strong>Undertow</strong>: a cone of water, 8 blocks (12 in the water), 6 damage, carries enemies away and slows them.</li>
<li><strong>Tail Sweep</strong>: the tail strikes all around within 3.5 blocks, 10 damage on land, 18 in the water, and throws away.
    <strong>ALT Tail Leap</strong>: a bound on the tail, 8 blocks (12 when you leap from the water); on landing the tail strikes
    around for 10.</li>
<li><strong>Siren Song</strong>: you sing for 5 s; enemies within 8 blocks of you are slowed and weakened while they hear it, and
    creatures forget their target. You strike no blow while you sing, and a hit does not stop the song. Press again to
    stop singing, for a shorter cooldown.</li>
<li><strong>Sea Escort</strong>: allies within 6 blocks (players who are not your enemies, your pets) swim like dolphins and breathe
    under water for 30 s.</li>
<li><strong>Princely Fist</strong>: a punch, 12 damage, the enemy cannot move for 1 s.</li>
<li><strong>Trident Throw</strong>: a trident of water thrown up to 30 blocks, 10 damage (15 with a trident in hand). <strong>ALT Trident
    Thrust</strong>: a thrust through every enemy on a 6-block line, 14 damage (21 with a trident).</li>
</ul>
<h2>The Modified Humans (new)</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Vinsmoke's bodies</strong>: a man's size, counted as a human for Rokushiki and styles. No Human Will.</li>
<li><strong>Modified Body</strong>: projectiles and cutting blows hurt 25 % less, 1 health back every 2 s, +2 bare hand damage, +10 %
  speed. Lovestruck and Negative never take hold, and the Conqueror's Haki knocks you out half as long.</li>
<li><strong>Unsociable</strong>, a flaw always on: villagers, wandering traders and the base mod's traders shake their head and will
  not trade with you. With Cruise Cruise no Mi, merchants and village heads refuse you too, and you win no reputation.</li>
<li><strong>The Germa 66 ration box</strong>: given once, the first time you are a modified human. Use it to put your suit on or take
  it off. <strong>Sneak and use</strong> opens a screen with the five suits: you in each one (drag to turn), its name and its seven
  techniques. Click one to wear it; the box remembers it. Only a modified human can use the box. A lost box can be
  crafted by a modified human (4 iron ingots, 4 redstone, a diamond; with Cruise Cruise no Mi, it is the Inventor's, level
  30) or bought from the base mod's pirate and marine traders for 5000 to 7500 Belly. The suit comes off when you die.</li>
<li><strong>The suits</strong> are worn on the body: belt, collar, gauntlets, boots and a cape with the number on its back. Poison
  Pink's cape is a pair of butterfly wings that beat. Nobody sees anything of you under Stealth.</li>
<li><strong>Raid Suit</strong> (any suit): fire hurts half as much, no fall damage, +4 bare hand damage.</li>
<li><strong>Seven techniques a suit</strong>, given while it is worn and gone when it comes off (a technique on your bar comes back to
  its slot). Every one has its own icon, pose and effects.</li>
<li><strong>Any suit</strong>: <strong>Germa Boots</strong> (the jets hold you up in the air for up to 8 s, then throw you in a big leap
    where you look) and <strong>Cape Shield</strong> (the cape spreads into a shield for 3 s and stops every attack from the front; press again to fold
    it early, for a shorter cooldown).</li>
<li><strong>Black Bug</strong> (red, blue and green): after a 1 s crouch, an 8-block rush through every enemy, 18 damage, +50 % for
    each other colour of suit that struck the same enemy in the last 2 s.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Sparking Red</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Sparking Figure</strong>: a 6-block rush that bursts on the first body, 16 damage and 10 around. <strong>ALT Double</strong>: a longer
  cast, 26 damage and 16 within 5 blocks, costs you 4 health.</li>
<li><strong>Sparking Valkyrie</strong>: five beams of light through everything, 25 blocks, 6 damage each.</li>
<li><strong>Sparking Blast</strong>: after a 1.5 s cast, a ball of light flies 30 blocks and bursts for 22 within 4 blocks.</li>
<li><strong>Sparking Flash</strong>: blinds and slows for 4 s those within 8 blocks who look at you.</li>
<li><strong>Black Bug</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Dengeki Blue</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dengeki Kick</strong>: a lightning leap onto the enemy you look at (10 blocks), 14 damage, paralysed 1.5 s and thrown.</li>
<li><strong>Henry Blazer</strong>: a 16-block zigzag, 12 damage (18 with a sword).</li>
<li><strong>Henry Needle</strong>: an electric needle, 30 blocks, 14 damage, paralysed 2 s.</li>
<li><strong>Henry Cracker</strong>: you rise into a storm that strikes 6 times for 6 within 5 blocks.</li>
<li><strong>Black Bug</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Winch Green</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Winch Arm</strong>: the gauntlet flies out on its steel cable, grabs the enemy and pulls it to you. <strong>ALT Winch
  Grapple</strong>: the hand grabs the block or body you look at (20 blocks) and pulls you there.</li>
<li><strong>Winch Punch</strong>: the fist on its cable, 16 blocks, 14 damage, thrown far.</li>
<li><strong>Winch Danton</strong>: grabs an enemy and swings it round, 12 damage to each body it hits.</li>
<li><strong>Winch Bomber</strong>: a series of jabs, then a wave along a 10-block line, 20 damage. Your arms come down after it.</li>
<li><strong>Black Bug</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Stealth Black</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Stealth</strong>: you vanish; your first blow does double damage.</li>
<li><strong>Silent Steps</strong> (passive): while hidden, no step is heard and mobs lose you.</li>
<li><strong>Stealth Rescue</strong>: the one beside you vanishes too, without ending your Stealth.</li>
<li><strong>Stealth Ambush</strong>: under Stealth, you appear behind the enemy you look at (12 blocks) and kick, 18 damage, doubled by
  Stealth to 36.</li>
<li><strong>Stealth Decoy</strong>: an image of you stands where you were and mobs go after it for 6 s, while your Stealth starts.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Poison Pink</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pink Hornet</strong>: a ball of poison that bursts into a cloud (3 blocks, 5 s), 12 damage and Poison II for 8 s. <strong>ALT
  Pink Kosen</strong>: after a 1 s cast, a ray of poison from the palm, 24 blocks, 20 damage and Poison III for 14 s.</li>
<li><strong>Pink Rasen</strong>: a poison tornado around you for 3 s draws enemies in, strikes 4 times for 6 with Poison II, then
  throws them up.</li>
<li><strong>Poison Mist</strong>: a mist of 6 blocks for 6 s, enemies in it are poisoned and slowed, you heal 1 a second.</li>
<li><strong>Poison Draw</strong>: draws every poison and bad effect out of the one you touch and heals them 8; looking at no one, on
  yourself.</li>
<li><strong>Poison Touch</strong> (passive): your bare-handed blows give Poison II.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Oni, Tontatta and Lunarians: two more techniques each</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Oni</strong>: <strong>Demon Sweep</strong> (a swing all around, 9 damage within 3 blocks, x1.2 with a blunt weapon) and <strong>Demon Quake</strong>
  (strikes the ground, everything within 4 blocks takes 8 and is thrown up; from the ground only).</li>
<li><strong>Tontatta</strong>: <strong>Ant Burden</strong> (lifts the nearest enemy over your head, even one much bigger, and throws it ahead, 8
  damage when it lands) and <strong>Tonta Whirl</strong> (two turns around the enemy in 1 s, 4 blows of 1, then nausea and
  blindness for 3 s).</li>
<li><strong>Lunarians</strong>: <strong>Flameless Rush</strong> (flame out only: an 8-block rush, the first enemy met takes 10 and stops you) and
  <strong>Wing Gust</strong> (your wings throw back everything 6 blocks ahead, 4 damage, and burn 2 s with the flame lit; not with
  the wings hidden).</li>
</ul>
<h2>Giants</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Shoulder Carry</strong> (every giant): right-click with an empty hand on a crouching player at least twice smaller than you
  to take them on your shoulder, one at a time. They can hit and use their techniques from there, but not on you, and
  you do not hurt them. They get down by crouching again, their fall spared; a crouching giant sets them down with an
  empty right-click.</li>
<li><strong>Higher steps</strong>: a giant steps a third of his height: Wotan 1.5 blocks, Elbaf 2.4, Ancient 3.6. A giant wading in
  water can jump, and climbs out onto a bank no higher than his step.</li>
<li><strong>The Ancient Giant</strong> breaks and places 5x5x5 blocks, so he fits the hole he digs (the Elbaf and the Wotan keep
  3x3x3). His skin is red, green or your own, a third each: some players change colour once.</li>
<li><strong>Continent Puller</strong> hits for 24 damage instead of 8, the strongest giant technique.</li>
<li><strong>Two new horn shapes</strong> for the Ancient Giant and the Oni: Harald's (long, thin, almost straight) and Oars' (from the
  temples). Some players who already had horns get another shape once.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Tontatta</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Tontatta Strength</strong> now gives a jump of 4.25 blocks (a man 1.25) and 15 % more speed, and a fall of 6 blocks
  unhurt, then a quarter of a man's damage a block: a fall of about 46 blocks kills a Tontatta, not 23.</li>
<li><strong>Small bodies</strong> (Tontatta and anyone smaller than a man): you no longer see through the walls of a corner, the
  third-person camera stays out of the walls, and you can stay at the surface of the water to breathe.</li>
<li>The book says it: less health than a man, but blows as strong.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Commands</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong><code>/ability unlock_group</code></strong> has a group for each race and sub-race (<code>racenomi:giant</code>, <code>racenomi:wotan</code>...): a race
  gives its whole kit, a sub-race only its own. A technique given by a command is no longer taken back by the race
  check.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Fixes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Changing race in the character creator</strong> no longer keeps the old sub-race's size, horns or body: an Ancient Giant
  turned Tontatta is a Tontatta's size.</li>
</ul>
</div>

## 0.2.0 (beta) { #v0-2-0 }

<small>Released 2026-11-02 · [Download](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/race-race-no-mi/files/9405200)</small>

<div class="changelog-body" markdown="0">
<p><strong>Three new races and three peoples of giants.</strong> The Oni, the Tontatta and the Lunarians join the sky people, the
fishmen, the humans and the giants, each with their own body and their own techniques. The giants now come as Elbaf
Giants, Wotan and Ancient Giants, and get two new techniques. Every icon of the sky people and the giants is redrawn.</p>
<p>Requires Minecraft <strong>1.20.1</strong>, Forge <strong>47+</strong>, <strong>Mine Mine no Mi 0.11.6</strong> and <strong>AkumaLib 6.3.0</strong> or newer.
<a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-sky-island">Mine Mine no Mi: Sky Island</a> is optional.
Needed on both the client and the server.</p>
<hr />
<h3>The Oni (new)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Nearly twice a man's height</strong> (size 1.75), with horns whose shape and colour are your own. Counts as a human for
  Rokushiki. Hold sneak to bend through a door.</li>
<li><strong>Oni Blood</strong>: harder bare hands and a tougher body. Drunk makes you stronger instead of sick: more damage and less
  damage taken for each level, up to level 5.</li>
<li><strong>Five techniques</strong>, bare-handed, stronger with a blunt weapon: Guard Breaker (breaks a raised shield), Horn Toss,
  Shock Fist (a shock wave in a line), Demon Skin (less damage taken, your blows push harder) and Demon Leap (a leap onto
  the enemy in sight, with a shock wave where you land).</li>
</ul>
<h3>The Tontatta (new)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>About 45 cm</strong> (size 0.25), with a round bushy tail in your hair's colour and, for some, a pointed nose. You go
  through slits a man cannot, and enemies have a hard time seeing and hitting you.</li>
<li><strong>Tontatta Strength</strong>: a man's blows and push, and you reach blocks as far as a man.</li>
<li><strong>Five techniques</strong>: Tonta Uppercut sends a man flying up, Underfoot Dash runs between legs, Tonta Barrage is twelve
  quick blows, Tail Hammer leaps onto an enemy, and Head Climb runs up a big body to strike its head.</li>
</ul>
<h3>The Lunarians (new)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>One and a half times a man</strong> (size 1.5), brown skin, white hair and long black wings. Fire and lava do not burn you.</li>
<li><strong>The flame on your back</strong>: lit, you take half the damage and every technique works. Out, you are a quick fighter:
  faster on foot and with your blows, harder bare hands, a higher jump, but only Flame Switch works. Water puts it out.</li>
<li><strong>Techniques</strong>: Flame Switch, Black Wings (8 s of flight, then you fall), Flame Blast, Flaming Blow and Flame Shroud.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Giants</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Three peoples to choose from</strong>: the Elbaf Giant (size 4, as before), the Wotan (size 2.5, an orange fin, swims and
  sees under water, takes less from blunt blows) and the Ancient Giant (size 6, horns and red or green skin, tougher and
  never pushed back, with Continent Puller). A giant made before this version stays an Elbaf-sized giant.</li>
<li><strong>Elbaf Charge</strong>: a charge straight ahead that throws small enemies aside.</li>
<li><strong>Roar of Elbaf</strong>: small enemies around you are slowed and weakened, you and your allies get stronger.</li>
<li><strong>Giant's Stomp</strong> reaches from the edge of your body, and hits what stood just beside a big giant.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Fixes</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>A technique of your old race</strong> no longer comes back after you change race. Its points are given back, as the base
  mod does.</li>
<li><strong>Sky dials</strong> keep their bonus every time in singleplayer.</li>
<li><strong>The race faces</strong> in the character creator no longer have their horns and hats cut off.</li>
<li><strong>Icons</strong>: every icon of the sky people and the giants is redrawn.</li>
</ul>
</div>

## 0.1.1 (beta) { #v0-1-1 }

<small>Released 2026-10-30 · [Download](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/race-race-no-mi/files/9376688)</small>

<div class="changelog-body" markdown="0">
<p><strong>Fishmen that look better and giants that bend in a hybrid.</strong> The fishmen's fins have some thickness and their skins more detail, and the flickering on their arms is gone. A hunched giant's hybrid form now bends with him. No number of the races has changed.</p>
<p>Requires Minecraft <strong>1.20.1</strong>, Forge <strong>47+</strong>, <strong>Mine Mine no Mi 0.11.6</strong> and <strong>AkumaLib 6.1.0</strong> or newer.
<a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-sky-island">Mine Mine no Mi: Sky Island</a> is optional.
Needed on both the client and the server.</p>
<hr />
<h3>Fishmen</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>No more flicker</strong>: the grey patch that flickered on the Saw-shark's and the Garfish's arm is gone, and so is the flicker of the skin colour.</li>
<li><strong>Fins with some thickness</strong>: fins, lobes, crest and tail are real slabs now, no longer flat. The saw has its teeth on both sides, and the gills are small ridges on the neck.</li>
<li><strong>The Ray's arm fin</strong> no longer floats behind the arm.</li>
<li><strong>Finer skins</strong>: lit rims and rays on the fins, the Ray's pale spots, the Garfish's scales.</li>
<li><strong>Your skin colour in other mods' forms</strong>: in a form that has its own skin, such as Missing Missing no Mi's King Cobra hybrid, you wear the form's skin, then your fishman colour again when you leave it.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Giants</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hunched in a hybrid</strong>: the form bends with your body. Your bent body no longer comes out of it.</li>
<li><strong>Tails</strong> lie as they do when you stand, hunched or on all fours, instead of pointing at the sky.</li>
</ul>
</div>

## 0.1.0 (beta) { #v0-1-0 }

<small>Released 2026-10-29 · [Download](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/race-race-no-mi/files/9362166)</small>

<div class="changelog-body" markdown="0">
<p><strong>The first beta: play the sky people, the fishmen, the humans and the giants of One Piece.</strong> Pick your race and
sub-race in Mine Mine no Mi's character creator. Each one has its own body, its own passive and, for the sky people
and the giants, a branch of techniques of its own in the abilities tree.</p>
<p>Requires Minecraft <strong>1.20.1</strong>, Forge <strong>47+</strong>, <strong>Mine Mine no Mi 0.11.6</strong> and <strong>AkumaLib 6.0.0</strong> or newer.
<a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mine-mine-no-mi-sky-island">Mine Mine no Mi: Sky Island</a> is optional.
Needed on both the client and the server.</p>
<hr />
<h3>Sky People</h3>
<p>Three sub-races: <strong>Skypiean</strong>, <strong>Birkan</strong> and <strong>Shandia</strong>. Wings on their back, and they count as humans for
Rokushiki.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Stronger the higher they stand</strong>: their passive grows up to x1.5 from height 128 to 256, and is always full in
  Sky Island's Skypiea.</li>
<li><strong>Skypiean Body</strong>: +20 % speed. <strong>Birkan Body</strong>: +1 damage, +1 toughness. <strong>Shandia Body</strong>: +3 damage, +2 toughness.</li>
<li><strong>Dials</strong>: Mine Mine no Mi's Axe, Flame, Impact, Milky and Reject Dials hit 25 % harder and recharge 25 % faster in
  their hands (40 % for a Skypiean).</li>
<li><strong>Mantra</strong>: their Observation Haki grows faster and reaches farther (x1.25, x1.5 for a Birkan).</li>
<li><strong>Techniques</strong>, in a branch of the tree for the sky people only:</li>
<li>for all: <strong>Axe Slash</strong> (a blade of air), <strong>Impact</strong> (catch half of each blow, then let it out in a shockwave),
    <strong>Sky Leap</strong> (three bounds through the air, with wings);</li>
<li>Skypiean: <strong>Breath Gale</strong> (a gust that throws back enemies and shots), <strong>Dial Blend</strong> (a blinding shock or a jet
    of fire);</li>
<li>Birkan: <strong>Mantra Mark</strong> (a target seen through walls, the next blow on it x1.5), <strong>Divine Stride</strong> (a dash
    through enemies, with wings);</li>
<li>Shandia: <strong>War Cry</strong> (Strength for you and your allies), <strong>Berserker</strong> (more damage and attack speed, less
    armour).</li>
<li>A real dial carried makes the dial techniques stronger.</li>
<li>Operators can take a Birkan's wings off with <code>/racenomi wings remove &lt;player&gt;</code>: a <strong>Wingless Birkan</strong> loses the
  wing techniques and gets the strongest Mantra (x2) and more strength.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Fishmen</h3>
<p>Mine Mine no Mi's <strong>Saw-shark</strong>, <strong>Ray</strong> and <strong>Garfish</strong> made whole: a look of their own (fins, snout, skin colour of
the sub-race, in first person too) and a passive each.</p>
<ul>
<li>Fishmen are bigger than a man: 1.25 times a man's size, with the size slider.</li>
<li><strong>Fishman Body</strong>: swims much faster; under water, endless air, night vision and haste.</li>
<li><strong>Saw-shark Body</strong>: harder blows, and a bare-fisted blow may make the target bleed. <strong>Ray Body</strong>: a karate master, harder
  and faster blows with bare hands. <strong>Garfish Body</strong>: the fastest swimmer.</li>
<li>Arlong, Kuroobi and Chew have their sub-race's passive too.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Humans</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Human Will</strong>: +25 % Doriki and Haki experience. Humans grow faster than anyone.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Giants</h3>
<p>The people of Elbaf: <strong>four times a man's size</strong> (about 7 blocks high), with a narrow size slider. Their reach,
blows, health and strides are a giant's, and so are their techniques. They do not count as humans for Rokushiki.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>A giant in a human world</strong>: he hunches under a low roof and crawls lower down (no techniques on all fours), walks
  through leaves, bamboo, vines and cobwebs, wades through water shallower than him, and goes through a portal as soon
  as he touches it.</li>
<li><strong>Blocks by zones</strong>: he breaks and places blocks by 3x3x3 (one item per block placed). The <strong>G</strong> key switches to
  precision mode, one block at a time, with his long reach. Ability griefing and protected areas are followed.</li>
<li><strong>Rest and storage</strong>: he sleeps on a 3x3 of hay or wool, and opens chests from his reach.</li>
<li><strong>Pride of Elbaf</strong> (passive): weakness and despair do not take hold of him, and below 30 % health he hits 25 %
  harder.</li>
<li><strong>Techniques</strong>, in a branch of the tree for giants only: <strong>Stomp</strong> (those around are hurt and thrown up),
  <strong>Giant's Grip</strong> (a man-sized body seized, then thrown), <strong>Boulder Throw</strong> (a boulder of real ground torn out,
