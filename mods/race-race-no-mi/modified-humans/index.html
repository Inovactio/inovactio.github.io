    Modified Humans¶

    The Vinsmoke's bodies: changed before birth, tough and fast, with no emotions, and the five Raid Suits of Germa 66.
    Size 1, a man's (slider 0.75 to 1.25)
    Passives Modified Body, Raid Suit, and a flaw: Unsociable
    Techniques 7 a suit, given while it is worn
    In the abilities menu their own category
    Rokushiki modified humans count as humans, for Rokushiki and the styles

    A modified human has no Human Will: their growth is a man's.

    In 3D: drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom. The buttons switch between the five suits.

    Modified Body¶

    Passive, modified humans

    The body of the user was changed before birth: a skin hard as a shell against bullets and blades, wounds that close fast, and more strength and speed than a man. With no emotions, nothing plays on their mind.

    Stat Value
    Projectile and cutting damage taken -25 %
    Health back 1 every 2 s
    Bare hand damage +2
    Speed +10 %

    Lovestruck and Negative never take hold, and the Conqueror's Haki knocks them out half as long.

    Unsociable¶

    Passive, a flaw always on

    Nobody warms to the user. Villagers, wandering traders and the base mod's traders shake their head and will not trade with them. With Cruise Cruise no Mi, merchants and village heads refuse them too, and they win no reputation.

    The Germa 66 ration box¶

    The box is given once, the first time the player is a modified human. Only a modified human can use it.

    • Use it to put the suit on, or take it off.
    • Sneak and use opens a screen with the five suits: the player in each one (drag to turn them), its name and its seven techniques. Click one to wear it; the box remembers it.
    • A lost box can be crafted by a modified human: 4 iron ingots, 4 redstone and a diamond. With Cruise Cruise no Mi it is the Inventor's, at level 30. The base mod's pirate and marine traders sell one for 5,000 to 7,500 Belly.
    • The suit comes off when the player dies.

    The suits are worn on the body: belt, collar, gauntlets, boots and a cape with the number on its back. Poison Pink's cape is a pair of butterfly wings that beat. Nobody sees anything of the player under Stealth.

    Raid Suit¶

    Passive, while a suit is worn

    The cloth bears fire, the boots land any fall and make the blows hit harder.

    Stat Value
    Fire damage taken -50 %
    Fall damage none
    Bare hand damage +4

    The suits and their techniques¶

    Each suit gives seven techniques while it is worn: the two of every suit, and five of its own. They go when it comes off, and a technique on the bar comes back to its slot. They are not bought in the abilities tree. Every one has its own icon, pose and effects.

    About the values

    Damage is what the target really loses, in health points (2 = one heart), and ranges are in blocks, for a body of size 1. Cooldowns and times are in seconds.

    Every suit¶

    Technique
    Germa Boots the jets hold the user up, then throw them in a big leap
    Cape Shield the cape stops every attack from the front

    Germa Boots¶

    Active, every suit

    The jets in the boots hold the user up in the air where they are. Used again, or when the time runs out, the jets throw them in a big leap where they look.

    Stat Value
    Cooldown 6 s
    Hold up to 8 s

    Cape Shield¶

    Active, every suit

    The cape spreads into a shield in front of the user and stops every attack from the front. Press again to fold it early, for a shorter cooldown.

    Stat Value
    Cooldown 12 s
    Hold 3 s

    Black Bug¶

    Active, red, blue and green suits

    After a 1 s crouch, the user rushes 8 blocks ahead through every enemy on the way. Each is hit harder for each other colour of suit that struck it in the last 2 s.

    Stat Value
    Cooldown 15 s
    Cast 1 s
    Damage 18, +50 % for each other colour

    Sparking Red¶

    Technique Cooldown
    Sparking Figure 10 s (Double 20 s) a 6-block rush that bursts on the first body: 16 damage, and 10 around. Alt: Double Sparking Figure, a longer cast, 26 damage and 16 within 5 blocks, costs the user 4 health
    Sparking Valkyrie 12 s five beams of light from the eyes, one after the other, through everything: 25 blocks, 6 damage each
    Sparking Blast 20 s after a 1.5 s cast, a ball of light flies 30 blocks and bursts for 22 within 4 blocks
    Sparking Flash 15 s those within 8 blocks who look at the user are blinded and slowed for 4 s
    Black Bug 15 s

    Dengeki Blue¶

    Technique Cooldown
    Dengeki Kick 8 s a lightning leap onto the enemy looked at (10 blocks): 14 damage, paralysed 1.5 s and thrown
    Henry Blazer 12 s a 16-block zigzag through every enemy passed: 12 damage, 18 with a sword in hand
    Henry Needle 10 s an electric needle, 30 blocks: 14 damage, paralysed 2 s
    Henry Cracker 25 s the user rises into a storm that strikes 6 times for 6 within 5 blocks
    Black Bug 15 s

    Winch Green¶

    Technique Cooldown
    Winch Arm 6 s the gauntlet flies out on its steel cable (15 blocks), grabs the enemy and pulls it to the user. Alt: Winch Grapple, the hand grabs the block or body looked at (20 blocks) and pulls the user there
    Winch Punch 8 s the fist on its cable, 16 blocks: 14 damage to the first enemy, thrown far
    Winch Danton 18 s grabs an enemy by the head and swings it round for 3 s: 12 damage to each body it hits, then thrown where the user looks. Hits on the user make them let go
    Winch Bomber 18 s a series of jabs, then a wave along a 10-block line: 20 damage. The arms come down after it
    Black Bug 15 s

    Stealth Black¶

    Technique Cooldown
    Stealth 20 s the user vanishes, with all they hold, up to 30 s; their first blow does double damage and shows them again
    Silent Steps passive while hidden, no step is heard and mobs lose the user
    Stealth Rescue 20 s the one beside the user vanishes too, for as long as they stay near, without ending the user's Stealth
    Stealth Ambush 12 s under Stealth, the user appears behind the enemy looked at (12 blocks) and kicks: 18 damage, doubled by Stealth to 36
    Stealth Decoy 25 s an image of the user stands where they were and mobs go after it for 6 s, while their Stealth starts

    Poison Pink¶

    Technique Cooldown
    Pink Hornet 8 s (Kosen 14 s) a ball of poison that bursts into a cloud (3 blocks, 5 s): 12 damage and Poison II for 8 s. Alt: Pink Kosen, after a 1 s cast, a ray of poison from the palm, 24 blocks: 20 damage and Poison III for 14 s
    Pink Rasen 18 s a poison tornado around the user for 3 s draws enemies in (4 blocks), strikes 4 times for 6 with Poison II, then throws them up
    Poison Mist 25 s a mist of 6 blocks for 6 s: enemies in it are poisoned and slowed, the user heals 1 a second
    Poison Draw 15 s draws every poison and bad effect out of the one the user touches and heals them 8; looking at no one, on the user
    Poison Touch passive bare-handed blows give Poison II
    Animations

    Taken in game.
