    Changelog¶

    Every Sky Island release for Minecraft 1.20.1, newest first, as published on CurseForge.

    0.2.0 (beta)¶

    Released 2026-09-26 · Download

    Skypiea comes alive: its first animals. Giant South Birds that always face south, two birds you can tame, saddle and fly, the Cloud Fox and the huge Giant Dog as companions - the dog a mount too. And a round of fixes: every village island gets its Heaven's Gate, geysers keep their vent, and exploring Skypiea no longer floods the log.

    Requires Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47+, Mine Mine no Mi 0.11.5 and AkumaLib 2.6.1 or newer (was 2.5.0). Client and server.

    ⚠️ Still a beta: worlds are generated from this version's rules. Chunks already explored keep what they have; islands you have not reached yet may get a Heaven's Gate they would not have had in 0.1.0 (see Fixes).

    New: the birds of Skypiea

    • The giant South Bird (Upper Yards): the Jaya bird, grown huge on Skypiea and friendly. A living compass - once it stops, it turns to face south, beak and all.
    • The Sky Dot Bird (Pierre's kind) - pink and dotted, rare on the Angel Islands and in the Upper Yards - and the Three-Jo Bird (Fuza's kind) - a great lilac vulture, rarer still, in the Upper Yards only. Both are flying mounts:
      • tame them with seeds (like a parrot), then put a vanilla saddle on them;
      • only their owner rides and steers them;
      • fly like a horse that flies: the movement keys go the way you look, Jump climbs, Sneak descends, with neither they hover;
      • in the air, Sneak never drops you off - land first, then Sneak to get down;
      • fall out of Skypiea on one and you both go home, still in the saddle.
    • Their wings fold on the ground, beat fast when hovering or climbing, slow when cruising, and hold still in a dive; they bank into turns - with a rider too.
    • Drops: feathers and meat (cooked if they burned), more with Looting; a mount's saddle drops too.

    New: companions

    • The Cloud Fox (Su's kind), on the Angel Islands: tame it with fish. It follows you, and lies down on the cloud when told to sit.
    • The Giant Dog (Holy's kind), very rare in the Upper Yards, huge: tame it with raw meat.
      • A guard, like a wolf: it goes for whatever strikes you and whatever you strike (never creepers, ghasts or your other animals), and catches up with you when it gets stuck in the trees.
      • A mount: saddle it and ride it like a horse - Jump leaps about three blocks. No fall damage under ten blocks.
      • 80 health, 8 attack damage.
    • Five coats each, the first the manga's own: fox cloud, sky, dawn, mint, dusk; dog cream, russet, silver, chocolate, charcoal.

    Fixes

    • Geysers keep their vent: an amethyst geode next to a geyser could crush it. A geyser now repairs its crater by itself.
    • Heaven's Gate on every village island: about half of them had none. Where the gate needs the room, the beach's deck chairs, castle or workshop are left out.
    • You always arrive in front of the gate, never on its roof, even on a sloping coast.
    • Valkyrien Skies: a ship arriving by geyser no longer lands on Heaven's Gate; it looks further out for open cloud sea where the sea is shallow.
    • Exploring Skypiea is smoother: the game no longer fills its log with warnings each time new land appears, which slowed it down. The islands themselves are unchanged.

    Known issues

    • Some Angel Islands have no White Beret lookout, or one oddly placed.
    • The Shandias' hidden village sometimes sits partly on an island instead of on the sea.
    • Some small Upper Yards have a single Shandia camp.
    • In a few worlds, leaves block the Giant Jack's ramp.

    0.1.0 (beta)¶

    Released 2026-09-24 · Download

    The first release. A Forge addon for Mine Mine no Mi that adds Skypiea as a dedicated dimension, reached by riding a Knock-Up Stream up from the floor of the deep oceans: the cloud sea, the Angel Islands and their Skypiean villages, the beach, Heaven's Gate, and the Upper Yards with the Giant Jack, the ruins of Shandora and the Shandias.

    Requires Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47+, Mine Mine no Mi 0.11.5 and AkumaLib 2.5.0 or newer. Client and server.

    ⚠️ The base mod's own sky islands no longer generate in the overworld: Skypiea replaces them. Structures already generated in an existing world stay where they are.

    ⚠️ A beta: worlds are generated from this version's rules. A later version may change where islands and structures stand in chunks not yet explored.

    The way up: the Knock-Up Stream

    • A geyser on the floor of the deep oceans, one for each island above. Every ten minutes it rumbles for thirty seconds, then erupts for ninety (all configurable).
    • The column throws whatever is caught in it into the sky - players, mobs, dropped items, boats with their riders - and sends it on to Skypiea. An eruption always lasts long enough to carry somebody from the sea floor to the top.
    • You arrive on the ground or on the cloud sea, never inside a block: in front of Heaven's Gate on an island with a village.
    • /locate structure inosky:knock_up_stream finds one. /geyser tells where the nearest one is and when it erupts; /geyser now makes it erupt at once (operators).

    The way down

    • Step off an island and sink through the cloud sea: below the bottom of Skypiea you come out high above the same place in the overworld, with slow falling. Boats come back too.

    Skypiea

    • Islands only above the overworld's deep oceans; open cloud sea everywhere else above deep water, nothing above land.
    • The cloud sea: a sea of cloud you swim in, with a swell, bluish and animated so it is never mistaken for island cloud. Boats float and sail on it.
    • Its own sky, and no lava sea under it.
    • Island cloud, sea cloud and the Knock-Up Core are the dimension's own blocks; the Upper Yards are made of the overworld's earth and stone - the Vearth of the Blue Sea.

    Angel Islands

    Two islands in three: low islands of island cloud from top to bottom, a few trees rooted in the cloud. * Skypiean villages: houses of cloud brick, cut cloud and shell tiles, streets and a square, Skypiean civilians and traders (the base mod's own). Larger villages on the larger islands, with an inn, a bandstand, kitchen gardens and a cloud fountain. * Cloud farmland: a hoe tills island cloud, and crops grow in it exactly as in farmland. * The White Berets: their post in every village (with a rare chest of seized goods), recruits and a captain policing the village - calibrated against the base mod's NPCs. * The village blocks can be crafted: cloud brick, cut cloud, cloud pillars, shell tiles, awning cloth, cloud glass, conch lanterns, Vearth urns. * Angel Beach: the flat shore at the foot of the cliffs. Dials wash up on it over time. A seafront once per village island - the dial workshop, a promenade, a pier, deck chairs, sometimes a cloud castle - and one to three White Beret lookouts along the shore. * Heaven's Gate: the way into Skypiea on every village island - the gate, its tunnel, and a causeway to the promenade.

    Upper Yards

    One island in three, rarer and far larger: jungle plains around y 100, and massifs rising to about 215. * The Giant Jack (one Upper Yard in three): a beanstalk climbable to the top, a ramp up its whole height, and the Golden Bell at the summit with its chest - one or two boxes, a Golden Box in about two chests in five. * Shandora, the City of Gold (a little over half of the Upper Yards, sometimes beside a Giant Jack): its square, avenues and halls, the stepped temple and its altar, and the crypt under the Poneglyph - the treasure, one or two boxes a chest. Halls, altar and crypt all hold boxes now and then. * Small ruins of Shandora: one to three per Upper Yard in the jungle - an altar, a colonnade, a fallen house, a sacred well. * The Shandias: warriors and a chief guarding their land. They attack only looters - open their chests and they come for you. Their own weapons, the Shandia spear and the burn bazooka, and skills from dials (burn bazooka, impact, breath dash, sky step, the chief's reject). * Shandia war camps: two to four per Upper Yard, in clearings of the plains - four tipis round a fire, a totem, a weapon rack, a lookout. A chest in every tipi. * The hidden village: the Shandias' village in a pocket of cloud on the sea at the island's foot, opening on the sea - six tipis round a square, the statue of Kalgara, totems along the path, the chief by the fire.

    Compatibility

    • Worldgen mods: Skypiea reads the overworld's own biomes to find the deep oceans (minecraft:is_deep_ocean) and changes nothing in the overworld beyond the geyser.
    • Valkyrien Skies 2 (optional, 2.4.11 or newer): a ship in an erupting Knock-Up Stream rises to Skypiea with its crew, lands on open cloud sea and floats on it; a ship that falls out of Skypiea goes back to the overworld with its crew. Sky Island never requires VS.

    Configuration

    <world>/serverconfig/inosky-server.toml: * geyser: cycle_ticks (12000), eruption_ticks (1800), charge_ticks (600); * return: return_y (-32), the height below which a body falls back to the overworld; * angel_beach: dial_chance (2000), dial_cap (2), how often dials wash up and how many a stretch of beach holds.

    Known issues

    • An amethyst geode generated in a neighbouring chunk can crush a geyser's vent (#42).
