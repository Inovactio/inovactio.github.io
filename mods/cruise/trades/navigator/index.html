    Navigator¶

    The Navigator learns by discovering places: every chunk he has never been in, every new land and every new kind of place in his log book. At his bench, the Chart Table, he makes the tools that find the way: the Log Pose, the Barometer, the Sea Chart and the Eternal Pose. At the helm, he makes the Carpenter's boats go a little faster.
    Workstation Chart Table
    Advancements tab Navigation
    Levels 1 to 100; new lands and places up to level 100

    The Chart Table¶

    Made at a crafting table, from paper, a compass and planks (any planks):
    Paper Compass Paper
    Planks Planks Planks
    Planks Planks

    It faces you when placed and is mined with an axe. It is the Navigator's bench: his four recipes are made there.

    Charting recipes¶

    Makes Level XP Ingredients
    Log Pose 1 12 Compass, Glass Pane, Leather, Gold Nugget
    Barometer 20 50 Glass Bottle, 2 × Copper Ingot, Tempest Mouse, Redstone Dust
    Sea Chart 30 70 2 × Paper, Compass, Pumpkin Octopus
    Eternal Pose 50 110 Log Pose, Lens, Magnet, 2 × Gold Ingot

    Exploring¶

    A Navigator earns 2 XP for every chunk he has never been in, on foot, by boat or in the air, in any dimension. Going back over old ground pays nothing.

    The Log Book¶

    Open it with the Log Book button on the Navigator's page of the professions book. It has two sections, both filled for any player in the Overworld:

    • Lands: every Overworld biome you stand in (+25 Navigator XP for each new one).
    • Places: every kind of structure you stand in or come within 8 blocks of, vanilla's, Mine Mine no Mi's and other mods' (buried treasure excepted) (+50 Navigator XP for each new one).

    Each further tenth of a section you complete pays Belly to anyone, and XP to a Navigator, from 500 Belly and 50 XP at 10 % up to 100 000 Belly and 6 000 XP at 100 %. See Bestiary and logbook.

    Level 1: the Log Pose¶

    The Log Pose (does not stack) is a needle under glass that points to the nearest biome of a chosen kind, like a compass needle.

    • Use it to switch to the next kind of land your Navigator level opens: it searches and points to the nearest one ("Log Pose set on Forests: N blocks").
    • Use it crouching to search again for the same kind from where you stand.
    • It searches 2 400 blocks around (4 800 from Navigator level 25), with a 3-second cooldown.
    • Only a Navigator can set it; anyone can follow the needle.
    Kind of land Navigator level
    Forests, Beaches, Plains 1
    Deserts, Savannas, Taigas 10
    Jungles, Swamps, Badlands 20
    Mountains, Snowy lands 30
    Dark Forests, Cherry Groves 40
    Mushroom Fields 60
    Ice Spikes 80
    the Deep Dark (searched underground) 100

    Deserts, swamps, plains, snowy lands and mushroom fields also match other mods' biomes of the same kind.

    Level 20: the Barometer¶

    The Barometer (does not stack) reads the weather, in real minutes, for anyone who uses it:

    • "Fair weather, set for about N minutes"
    • "Clear for now - rain in about N minutes"
    • "Raining - it clears in about N minutes"
    • "Raining - a storm breaks in about N minutes"
    • "Storm - it passes in about N minutes"

    Where there is no sky (the Nether, the End) the needle does not move. It only forecasts: it does not change the weather.

    Level 30: the Sea Chart¶

    The Sea Chart (stacks to 16) is a blank chart.

    • Use it to pick the next kind of place your level opens; use it crouching to draw it.
    • It becomes a map ("Sea Chart: Villages") with the nearest such place marked: ocean monuments and woodland mansions with their vanilla icons, the rest with an X. It searches up to 1 600 blocks around.
    • Like a cartographer's map, a chart never leads to a place already charted in the world.
    • Drawing takes one blank chart, gives 20 Navigator XP, and has a 3-second cooldown.
    • Only a Navigator can draw one; the drawn map is anyone's to read, sell or give.
    Kind of place Navigator level What it finds
    Villages, Shipwrecks, Ocean Ruins, Pirate Ships, Training Grounds 30 villages, shipwrecks, ocean ruins; Mine Mine no Mi's small and medium pirate ships and the ghost ship; its training structures
    Marine Posts, Bandit Hideouts, Pillager Outposts, Temples 40 Mine Mine no Mi's small Marine bases and watch towers, its bandit structures; pillager outposts; desert and jungle pyramids
    Ruined Portals, Witch Huts, Igloos, Trail Ruins 50 the vanilla structures
    Marine Fortresses, Warships, Ocean Monuments 60 Mine Mine no Mi's large Marine base and large ships; ocean monuments
    Sky Islands 70 Mine Mine no Mi's sky islands
    Woodland Mansions 80 the vanilla structure
    Ancient Cities 90 the vanilla structure
    Strongholds 100 the vanilla structure

    Level 50: the Eternal Pose¶

    The Eternal Pose (stacks to 16, uncommon) points to one place, for good.

    • A Navigator uses a blank one to calibrate it where he stands (one off the stack; +40 Navigator XP). From then on it points there and cannot be set again.
    • It is named for the place: the structure he stood in (within 8 blocks), otherwise the biome, or whatever name an anvil gave it.
    • Anyone can use a calibrated one to see how far it is ("It points to X: N blocks away", or "in another world").

    At the helm¶

    From level 10, the Carpenter's boats you steer make a little more way, on any water (+1 %); from level 40, more again (+2 %, replacing the +1 %). The boat's total speed boost (hull, pattern, sail and helmsman together) is capped at ×1.075, so a Navigator makes an ordinary boat faster but adds little on top of the fastest one. See Boats and the Carpenter.

    Where the XP comes from¶

    Source XP
    A chunk never visited 2
    A new land in the log book 25
    A new kind of place in the log book 50
    Drawing a Sea Chart 20
    Calibrating an Eternal Pose 40
    Chart Table recipes 12 / 50 / 70 / 110
    Each tenth of a log book section 50 up to 6 000

    Level perks¶

    Level Perk What it does
    10 "Fair wind: the Carpenter's boats you steer make a little more way, on any water" the Carpenter's boats you steer go 1 % faster
    25 "Long sight: your Log Pose reaches twice as far" your Log Pose searches 4 800 blocks instead of 2 400
    40 "Trade winds: the boats you steer make more way again" the boats you steer go 2 % faster (replaces the 1 %), within the ×1.075 cap

    Advancements¶

    The Navigation tab opens at Navigator level 1: Navigation (become a Navigator), Apprentice Navigator (level 5), Which Way? (make a Log Pose), Storm Warning (make a Barometer), Charting the Seas (make a Sea Chart), A Way Home (make an Eternal Pose), The Whole World (every land in the log book) and Every Port of Call (every kind of place in the log book).
