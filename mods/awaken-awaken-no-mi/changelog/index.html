    Changelog¶

    Every Awaken Awaken no Mi release for Minecraft 1.20.1, newest first, as published on CurseForge. The Minecraft 1.16.5 releases (0.2.1 to 0.7.2) are listed there too.

    2.0.0¶

    Released 2026-09-18 · Download

    ⚠️ AkumaLib update required: 2.4.0

    This version needs AkumaLib 2.4.0 or newer. With an older AkumaLib the game will stop on Forge's missing-dependency screen.

    Just replace the AkumaLib jar in your mods folder with the new one.

    Every awakening is now complete. Every Paramecia and Logia awakening has at least three abilities, and every Zoan has all of its awakened forms plus two abilities beside them. This release adds 77 new awakened abilities, bringing the mod to 70 fruits and 225 awakened abilities.

    Every awakening was also gone over while its fruit was extended: numbers brought up to a fight against an armoured 250 HP player, promised mechanics that did nothing made real, missing sounds, poses and tooltips added, and icons redrawn where they did not read.

    New Content

    New Awakened Abilities

    Paramecia

    • Awa Awa no Mi: Bubble Cage, Squeaky Clean
    • Baku Baku no Mi: Armor Munch, Iron Stomach
    • Bane Bane no Mi: Ricochet
    • Bara Bara no Mi: Bara Bara Parade, Severed Grip
    • Beta Beta no Mi: Beta Beta Surge, Slick Passage
    • Chiyu Chiyu no Mi: Cellular Overgrowth, Overflow
    • Doa Doa no Mi: Backdoor
    • Doku Doku no Mi: Virulence
    • Doru Doru no Mi: Wick
    • Goe Goe no Mi: Broken Focus
    • Gura Gura no Mi: Aftershock
    • Hana Hana no Mi: Bloom Crusher, Clutch Lock
    • Hiso Hiso no Mi: Call of the Wild, Eavesdrop
    • Horo Horo no Mi: Ghost Legion, Phantom Payload
    • Horu Horu no Mi: Hell Wink, Iron Constitution
    • Kachi Kachi no Mi: Scalding Steam, Friction Burn
    • Karu Karu no Mi: Karmic Return
    • Kilo Kilo no Mi: Weight Shift
    • Kira Kira no Mi: Brilliant Meteor, Unscratchable
    • Kobu Kobu no Mi: Never Surrender
    • Kuku Kuku no Mi: Gourmet Metabolism
    • Mero Mero no Mi: Enthralling Gaze, Heart Rebound
    • Mini Mini no Mi: Lilliput Crush, Tiny Titan

    Zoan

    Every awakened Zoan's second form used to have no ability of its own; each one now does.

    • Hito Hito no Mi: Insight, Adaptive Mind
    • Hito Hito no Mi: Daibutsu: Sanctuary
    • Kame Kame no Mi: Snapping Jaw
    • Ryu Ryu no Mi: Allosaurus: Apex Pursuit
    • Ryu Ryu no Mi: Brachiosaurus: Neck Catapult
    • Ryu Ryu no Mi: Pteranodon: Downdraft
    • Sai Sai no Mi: Juggernaut
    • Sara Sara no Mi: Axolotl: Toxic Reserve, Toxic Bloom
    • Tori Tori no Mi: Phoenix: Rebirth Blaze
    • Ushi Ushi no Mi: Bison: Thundering Herd
    • Ushi Ushi no Mi: Giraffe: Neck Hammer
    • Zou Zou no Mi: Trunk Geyser
    • Zou Zou no Mi: Mammoth: Drought Siphon, Ancient Hide

    A Few Highlights

    • Trunk Geyser (Zou): a jet of seawater. A Devil Fruit user it soaks loses their powers for a moment, as if they had fallen into the sea
    • Rebirth Blaze (Phoenix): the phoenix burns, untouchable, and is reborn at full health - the health it takes back erupts as a nova. The lower it was, the bigger the blast
    • Thundering Herd (Bison): run a route, and a phantom stampede thunders down the whole of it
    • Ancient Hide (Mammoth): in an awakened form, no single blow takes more than 15% of your health
    • On the Beat (Oto): blows landed on the tempo build a damage combo
    • Anesthesia (Ope): a numbed target's invulnerability frames stop protecting it from you and your side

    Changes

    Renamed abilities

    Awakened abilities no longer borrow the names of techniques the manga gives to the fruit's normal form:

    • Entei → Crown of Flames, and Entei Flight → Crown Flight (Mera Mera)
    • Hiraishin → Stormstep (Goro Goro)
    • Kirimanjaro → Longneck Ram (Ushi Ushi: Giraffe)
    • Punk Corrida → Scrap Tempest (Jiki Jiki)
    • Takamagahara → Lightfall Domain (Pika Pika)
    • Yomi no Fubuki → Soulchill Aura (Yomi Yomi)

    Reworked awakenings

    Every awakening that already existed was revisited when its fruit was extended. Among the larger changes:

    • Damage raised across many abilities that were weaker than the fruit's own base kit - for example Drought Calamity, Ivory Catastrophe, Ao no Rakka, Horn Drill, Ancient Spine Saw and Slick Disarm
    • Mechanics that were promised and missing now work: Ivory Catastrophe actually spins, Candle World burns those caught in its soft wax, Spin (Kame) blocks attacks, Battlefield Resonance applies its bonuses, Kenzan World can no longer be dodged by crouching
    • Friend and foe sorted out: several zones no longer help enemies or hurt allies (Spring Domain, Waterpool Domain, Resonating World, High-Frequency Wall, Kenzan World)
    • Fiddle Overdrive (Bison): no longer knocks you unconscious when it ends, and its steam finally shows
    • Tooltips: many abilities now show their real numbers - heals, durations, cooldowns and bonuses that were missing
    • Every ability description rewritten in the base mod's register
    • Status effect icons redrawn as 18x18 pixel art, matching vanilla's

    Sounds

    • Dozens of abilities that were silent now have their own sounds
    • Zone charge sounds no longer play two or three copies of themselves on top of each other

    Fixes

    • Interrupted charges (water, Seastone, handcuffs) no longer deliver the ability at full power, and no longer leave you rooted or a zone half-built
    • Spring Domain: allies' jumps are no longer worse inside the field, bounces never lose their speed, and the zone costs a fraction of what it did
    • Spring Fajin: charging in mid-air holds you in place instead of dropping you
    • Drought Calamity: the charge animation no longer breaks in a released game, and the drought no longer drains caves deep under the field
    • Beta Beta mucus no longer slows the server down the more of it exists
    • Dislocated limbs hide correctly when both arms and legs are dislocated
    • Awakening smoke sits on the neck of several forms that had it misplaced

    Requirements

    • Minecraft 1.20.1
    • Forge 47.4.18+
    • Mine Mine no Mi 1.20.1-0.11.5
    • AkumaLib 2.4.0+

    1.1.0¶

    Released 2026-09-13 · Download

    ⚠️ AkumaLib update required: 2.2.0

    This version needs AkumaLib 2.2.0 or newer. With an older AkumaLib the game will stop on Forge's missing-dependency screen.

    Just replace the AkumaLib jar in your mods folder with the new one.

    Every Zoan awakening is now complete. The awakened Zoans only covered one of their two forms. This release adds the twelve missing awakened forms, gives the Daibutsu its first real ability, and brings the mod to 70 fruits and 148 awakened abilities.

    New Content

    Fruits Abilities

    • Hito Hito no Mi: Daibutsu: Added Nyorai Shinshō

    Awakened Zoan Forms

    Each awakened Zoan now has both of its forms, not just one. Every new form has its own model, stats and icon, and follows the same calibration as the awakened form the fruit already had.

    • Walk Points: Leopard, Allosaurus, Axolotl, Bison, Giraffe and Sai
    • Guard Points: Kame, Brachiosaurus, Mammoth and Zou
    • Fly Points: Phoenix and Pteranodon

    Nyorai Shinshō

    The Daibutsu awakening used to be just a form. Nyorai Shinshō brings the palm of the Buddha down from the sky onto the point you aim at, up to 64 blocks away:

    • 120 damage to everything within 14 blocks of the impact
    • Everything it lands on is sealed into the ground for 40 seconds, unable to walk, swim or jump
    • Where the base Impact Blast strikes forward from the fist, this one comes from above, at range, and holds

    Quality of Life

    • Your fruit's own abilities work in the new forms: every base ability that needs a Walk, Guard or Fly Point also accepts its awakened version. Kame Rideable, Brachio Bomber, Ancient Sweep, Mammoth Trample, Fujiazami and Flames of Regeneration included
    • Faster flight: the awakened Fly Points use the fruit's normal flight, only faster
    • Awakened Kame Guard Point: crouching retracts into the shell, where almost nothing gets through
    • Awakening smoke: on the new forms, the smoke around the neck follows the model's head

    Changes

    • Glittering Storm (Kira Kira): the diamond tornado is no longer put out by rain. Water still stops it

    Fixes

    • Titan Trample (Deka Deka), Spin (Kame Kame) and Brachio Stomp (Ryu Ryu: Brachiosaurus) no longer damage their own user

    Requirements

    • Minecraft 1.20.1
    • Forge 47.4.18+
    • Mine Mine no Mi 1.20.1-0.11.5
    • AkumaLib 2.2.0 — update required
    • (Recommended) Mine Mine no Mi: Awaken Path for survival progression

    1.0.0¶

    Released 2026-09-03 · Download

    ⚠️ New required dependency: AkumaLib

    From this version on, AkumaLib must be installed alongside this mod. Without it the game will stop on Forge's missing-dependency screen.

    AkumaLib is a library that adds no content of its own — it holds the shared framework this mod is built on. Just drop it in your mods folder.

    Every Devil Fruit in Mine Mine no Mi now has at least one awakening. This release adds 10 new fruits and 34 new abilities, taking the mod to 70 fruits and 135 awakened abilities.

    The Logia jumped from 3 fruits to 10.

    New Content

    Fruits Abilities

    • Goro Goro no Mi: Added Kami no Kuni, Hiraishin and Storm Sovereign
    • Pika Pika no Mi: Added Takamagahara, Kousoku and Sun's Blessing
    • Suna Suna no Mi: Added Sabaku no Okoku, Sabaku no Sousou and Kawaki
    • Moku Moku no Mi: Added White Night, White Ash and White Plague
    • Yuki Yuki no Mi: Added Yukiro, Gokkan and Kanpa
    • Gasu Gasu no Mi: Added Taiki, Asshuku and Chuwa
    • Yami Yami no Mi: Added Kyomu and Gluttony
    • Jiki Jiki no Mi: Added Assign World, Punk Corrida and Ferrous Dominion
    • Wara Wara no Mi: Added Harvest of Fate, Nail of Fate and Shared Fate
    • Zushi Zushi no Mi: Added Ukiyo and Zenith
    • Kage Kage no Mi: Added Shadow Marionette and Sunless Brand
    • Hie Hie no Mi: Added Hyosai and Hyoten
    • Magu Magu no Mi: Added Kagutsuchi and Netsuryo

    Passive Abilities

    Ten of the new abilities are passives — they work on their own, with no key to press, and grow stronger the more you play to the fruit's strengths.

    • Storm Sovereign (Goro Goro): stronger during a thunderstorm
    • Sun's Blessing (Pika Pika): stronger in bright light
    • Kawaki (Suna Suna): stronger standing on sand and quicksand
    • Ferrous Dominion (Jiki Jiki): stronger standing on your own scrap field
    • Zenith (Zushi Zushi): stronger the higher above the ground you are
    • Hyoten (Hie Hie): stronger the more enemies around you are frozen
    • Netsuryo (Magu Magu): banks heat from molten ground, spent by Kagutsuchi
    • Gluttony (Yami Yami): scales with the blocks you have swallowed
    • White Plague (Moku Moku), Kanpa (Yuki Yuki), Shared Fate (Wara Wara), Sunless Brand (Kage Kage): each extends its fruit's own loop

    Quality of Life

    • Animations: charged abilities now play a wind-up pose, so an attack can be seen coming
    • Sounds: new sound effects across the Moku, Yuki, Gasu, Suna, Hie and Magu kits

    Fixes

    • Ability descriptions: 18 abilities showed a raw translation key instead of their description, and 46 more had text that no longer matched what the ability did
    • Performance: fixed a lag source that ran on every block face on every chunk rebuild, caused by the invisible block system
    • Icons: reworked the Suke Suke no Mi icons, and redrawn Kousoku

    Requirements

    • Minecraft 1.20.1
    • Forge 47.4.18+
    • Mine Mine no Mi 1.20.1-0.11.5
    • AkumaLib 1.0.0 — new in this version
    • (Recommended) Mine Mine no Mi: Awaken Path for survival progression
